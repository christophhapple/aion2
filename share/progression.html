How progression works

Gear score opens content

Every dungeon has a gear score (GS) gate. GS comes from gear, enhancement, belt, amulet, Clash Runes, Manastones, Daevanion points and Arcana. Guides call it "item level"; it's the same number.

Enhance Stones are the limit

Each +1 enhancement gives +1 GS In-game, and +1 to +10 always succeeds Guides. So stones turn straight into GS.

Daevanion counts toward GS

Each Daevanion point you spend raises GS by 1 In-game. Pantheon reportedly raises only Combat Power (how strong you are), not GS Guides.

The plan from 1,000 to 1,400

Every week

  1. All 14 Daily Dungeon runs. About 90,000 stones a week with no Odyle cost. Nothing else comes close.
  2. Spend Odyle Energy before it hits the cap (560; it refills 120 a day). Use it on Krao and Draupnir Conquest cubes, or on the cheaper Krao and Urugugu Exploration cubes.
  3. Nightmare, 2 attempts a day; unused attempts carry over. Save the fragments for a spare Clash Rune.

Once

  1. Finish all Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds. Check under Map → Exploration tab.
  2. Do all regional (green) quests. They can't be repeated, and many give a skill point. Guides
  3. Clear each expedition's Conquest once: the achievement gives about 2,480 extra stones.
  4. Enhance all gear to +10. It always succeeds, and extracting old gear returns 100% of the stones (not the Kina) In-game.
  5. Belt and amulet to Epic +10, then transmute to Unique. Both are kept forever.

From 1,400 onward

  1. Urugugu and Vakron Conquest (2+ players) for the next gear set. Conquest pays Kina on the boss kill (about 300k each), so no Odyle is needed for that part. In-game
  2. Transcendence at 1600. Two dungeons, Deus Research Base and Shattered Arkanis, 2 clears a day each (up to 4 saved). They share the 10 weekly "unopened cube" allowances with Conquest. In-game Each drops Arcana for its own slots, and higher stages drop better grades. Guides
  3. Fill every Arcana slot, even with Common cards: each one is free GS. Upgrading cards needs no Enhance Stones and can't fail. Guides
  4. Use the Abyss time every week: sieges, corridors and the Abyss Command Scrolls (see routines). Unused hours are lost on Wednesday. In-game

Wings and flight time

  • Flight time comes from Flight Power, and every pair of wings you own adds to it, equipped or not. In-game
  • The four Daeva quest wings give the most: Lesser 2,500, Intermediate, Superior and Ultimate 1,000 each. Ultimate Daeva Wings come from a level-45 quest, so don't skip it. Other wings add about 200 each. In-game
  • Wings can't be enhanced on the European servers, whatever guides say. In-game
  • Gliding uses no Flight Power. Wind Serum (Alchemy) refills it in the air. Guides

Belt and Revelation amulet

  • Path: Rare +10 → transmute → Epic +10 → transmute → Unique +10 (the current maximum). Guides
  • Each +1 costs 1 scroll, Enhance Stones and a little Kina. In-game
  • Both start at Rare +0. Each item gets 30 scrolls in total, 10 per tier. A maxed Monolith (level 30) has given you all 30 amulet scrolls, so scrolls are rarely the limit; Enhance Stones are. In-game
  • Belt scrolls come from Strongholds; amulet scrolls only from Monolith levels. Guides
  • The Unique tier is expensive: about 200,000 stones and 5 million Kina from +0 to +10. Guides
  • Spare feathers after Monolith level 30: keep them. Korea can convert them into Enhance Stones; Europe can't yet.

Dates to remember

  • 25 Oct: clock change; the daily reset probably moves to 08:00 local.
  • 15 Dec: Daeva Pass Season 1 ends.
  • 16 Dec, 08:00: Season 1 ends. Spend all Oath Coins in the Season Shop before then; they don't carry over. Use season tickets and keys rather than hoarding them. Guides

Be careful with

  • Risk Clash Runes above +1: a failed upgrade destroys the rune In-game. Only try it with a spare rune. There are only 2 rune slots.
  • Risk Gear above +10: success drops from 65% to 20%. A failure costs only materials; the item survives. Guides
  • Membership raises the Odyle cap to 840 and the Shugo key cap to 21, and lets you open each reward cube twice (paying Odyle again) In-game. Extra Abyss time (14h, from guides) isn't on the in-game benefit list.
  • The Market needs membership to sell. Listing costs 2% (Bound Kina is used first) and a sale is taxed 10%, so you keep about 88%. In-game Without membership, crafting and gathering are for your own use.

The game is new, guides often contradict each other, and patches change things. Facts marked "In-game" were checked by a player in the game. If something here doesn't match what you see in the game, tell whoever shared this page.
