The loop

Daily Dungeon + Odyle cubes→ Enhance Stones→ All gear to +10→ Transcendence for Arcana→ Next gear score stage

Around this, every day: duties (they don't bank), Odyle before the cap, Shugo and Invasion keys before they reach their caps.

The game is new and guides often contradict each other. Facts marked "In-game" were checked by a player. If something doesn't match what you see, tell whoever shared this page.
