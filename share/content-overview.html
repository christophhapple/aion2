The bell next to an event plays three beeps 2 minutes before it starts. Alarms are saved in this browser only, and the page has to stay open (a background tab is fine, but the beep can then come up to a minute late).

The loop

Daily Dungeon + Odyle cubes→ Enhance Stones→ All gear to +10→ Transcendence for Arcana→ Next gear score stage

Around this, every day: duties (they don't bank), Odyle before the cap, Shugo and Invasion keys before they reach their caps, the Emergency Supply Requests. For Kina, run Conquest. For Abyss Points (Stigma Shards), the Abyss Corridor and the weekly Command Scrolls pay best.
