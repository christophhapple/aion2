## Skill levels
- **Where levels come from** (in-game 2026-10-11): skill points raise a skill to **Lv 10**; Lv 11–14 come
  from **Daevanion** board nodes; Lv 15–20 from **Arcana**. Leftover skill points can only go to passives.
- The skill window's tooltip shows Daevanion levels as a bonus: "Earth's Retribution (+4)" at Lv 14
  (in-game 2026-10-11).
- **Specialties (active skills):** **3 slots**, unlocking at skill Lv 8, 12 and 20 (in-game 2026-10-11;
  also [129][131]). You fill them from **5 options**: options 1–3 available from Lv 8, option 4 from
  Lv 12, option 5 from Lv 16 [133, snippet]. Confirmed in-game: options 4 and 5 are marked "12" and
  "16" and locked below that level (Scattershot at Lv 11; in-game 2026-10-11). This is what the older note
  "modifiers at 12 and 16" meant. Guides write picks as option numbers, e.g. "2, 4, 5" [132].
- Changing specialties: free in KR [134]; global cost not confirmed, but no one reports one.
- **Stigma specialties** unlock with Stigma level (5/10/15/20 [133]), no choices (in-game 2026-10-11).

## Stats (guides, 2026-10, unverified)
Guides disagree; most agree on the first point.
- **Attack is the main stat, even for healing:** the passive Healing Enhancement converts Attack into
  Heal Boost (rate not shown in-game) [111]. Another guide puts Healing Power first, then Attack [113].
- Then **Critical Hit** (feeds Condemnation resets; capped at 50%), **Cooldown Reduction** and
  **Combat Speed**, Double Chance [111][112]. A Cleric is "always short on crit" [112].
- Survival-first lists (Constitution, Willpower, HP, defenses) exist [114], but read like generic advice.
- Manastones: weapon → Weapon Damage Boost, Crit Damage Boost, Damage Boost, Attack; armor → one Crit
  Damage Boost, rest Attack; skip Front Attack [111].
- Daevanion: blue skill nodes first, then Cooldown Reduction + Combat Speed (Nezekan), Damage Boost/
  Tolerance (Zikel), Crit Damage (Vaizel) [111].
- Titles: see `titles.md`.
