# Cleric skills (active & Stigma)

Source: in-game tooltips, collected 2026-10-09 (imported from the Claude doc "AION 2 Cleric - Skills &
Build Notes"). Passives omitted (not customizable). Values only where the game showed them.

## Active

| Skill | Effect |
|---|---|
| Earth's Retribution | Single-target Earth damage, restores 110 MP. The MP builder. |
| Judgment Thunder | Wind damage to up to 4 enemies around the target |
| Debilitating Mark | AoE Wind damage; lowers enemy damage output; DoT 10s |
| Divine Aura | Stationary aura, periodic Earth damage for 5s |
| Chain of Torment | AoE Earth damage; lowers enemy damage tolerance; DoT 10s |
| Lightning Strike Scattershot | Damage to a Staggered target only |
| Light of Regeneration | Party HP regen every 2s for 30s |
| Condemnation | AoE Earth damage; requires Chain of Torment on target |
| Healing Light | Heals you + the lowest-HP party member (6s CD) |
| Radiant Recovery | Party heal; removes 1 debuff |
| Bolt | Chargeable AoE Wind damage (max 3 charge levels); 300 MP, 45s CD |
| Defiance | Breaks stun/knockdown/airborne/grab/frost/fear; 5s status immunity |

## Stigma

All Lv. 22, unlocked after the Ascension quest. 4 of 13 can be slotted.

| Skill | Effect |
|---|---|
| Power Burst | Big single-target Earth damage; heavy Stagger (50 gauge) |
| Absolution | Party heal; removes up to 2 debuffs |
| Benevolence | Party regen for 20s (90s CD) |
| Prayer of Amplification | +20% Attack for a duration |
| Summon Resurrection | Revives a party member (only revive in the game) |
| Earth Punishment | Earth damage + DoT 10s |
| Salvation | 3s immunity to damage and status effects |
| Root | AoE Earth damage; chance to Root (100% on NPCs) |
| Light of Protection | Toggle: party damage + defense buff (doesn't stack with Chanter's Undefeated Mantra) |
| Yustiel's Power | Party damage-tolerance buff for a duration |
| Voice of Doom | AoE Earth damage + DoT; cuts enemy healing 60% for 20s |
| Assault Mark | AoE damage; chance to Root; +Defense per target hit |
| Noble Aura | Follower aura that attacks your target for 5 min |

## Skill levels
Specialty slots unlock at skill level 8, 12 and 20; modifiers at 12 and 16. Respec is free.
Known specialty: Earth's Retribution → "-7s Bolt cooldown on Discharge".
