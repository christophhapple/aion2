# Kina

Researched 2026-10-14. No official global page; guides 2026-09 → 10, several written by Kina sellers
[258][265]. Unverified unless tagged `in-game`. Sources [n]: `sources.md`. Market rules: `crafting.md`.

## Bound vs normal Kina

- **Bound Kina:** locked to the character, can't be traded. Pays for enhancement, NPC shops, crafting,
  teleports. Not usable on the Market or for Quna [257][259][260]. The split came from a KR patch
  (2026-03) [265].
- **Normal Kina:** additionally Market, shared storage between characters, Kina Chests [257][261].
- **Bound is spent first** when both work (5 guides agree, nothing official) [257]–[261].
- **Which sources give which (contradictory):** Bound from quests, Duty Missions, NPC sales, Sealed
  Dungeons, Strongholds, achievements [259][260][265, KR]; normal from monster kills and boss Kina cubes
  [261][265]. Expedition cubes: normal [265, KR] vs Bound (Exploration) / normal (Conquest) [259].
- **Daily cap:** Kina from field monsters capped at **1,000,000 per character per day** [257][261][262].
- **For F2P (inference):** Market buying and the Kina ↔ Quna exchange are membership-only [263][264][266]
  [268], and selling needs membership (in-game 2026-10-13, `crafting.md`). So Bound or not hardly
  matters: Kina is only for your own progression.
- **With membership:** Market sales pay normal Kina (minus ~10% tax) [390][393]. Crafting for Kina:
  `crafting.md` → "Crafting for Kina".

## Sinks (largest first, inference from the numbers)

1. **Unique belt/amulet enhancement:** 297k–727k per step; Heroic belt 728k–1.43M [269].
2. **Arcana Transmutation:** 2M, +3M for the second line (the first line is free, max 2 lines; in-game 2026-10-19), weekly cooldown [269] (`gear.md`).
3. **Soul Fuse:** 1M per extra line [242] (`gear.md`).
4. Normal gear enhancement: +2 5,000 · +5 18,500 · +10 51,500 per step [269]. Kina from failed tries
   and extraction is lost.
5. Weekly: Command Scrolls 15,000 each (in-game), Substance Morph 50,000 per craft (max 20/week) [262],
   Soul Binding 30,000 per try [242].
6. Small: Duty reroll (1,000, rising) [262], Theostone engraving 10,000, teleports, repairs.

## Guide advice
- Upgrade the belt and amulet first; stop at +10 early (failed tries above +10 lose the Kina) [260][269].
- Enhance only gear you'll keep; keep a reserve for fees [257]. Don't let Odyle sit at the cap [257][258].
- Don't buy Kina from third-party sellers: ban risk [257][259][263].

## Buff scrolls (General Goods Merchant)

- **Sold by the General Goods Merchant** (in-game 2026-10-23), all Bound, Common, 5 min, 1s cooldown, 1,000 Kina,
  sell back for 10:
  - **Courage Scroll:** +10% Combat Speed.
  - **Speed Scroll:** +10% Move Speed.
  - **Benediction Scroll:** +10% Max HP and +100 Max MP.
  - Also teleports (prices not seen): **Return Scroll** (to the nearest activated Kibelisk, 3s cooldown) and
    **Hall Return Scroll** (to the Legion Hall).
- **Not sold by the merchant** (in-game 2026-10-23): Battle Enhance and Absorption.
- **Absorption Scroll** (shield, 1 min [421]): not found in any shop, merchant or premium (in-game 2026-10-23).
  Possibly KR-only or drop-only (unverified).
- **Battle Enhance Scroll (Bound):** +10% Damage Boost and +10% Damage Tolerance, 5 min, 1s cooldown. Premium
  shop: a stack of 10 for 4,000 of the paid currency (~20 USD worth, user 2026-10-23), limit 3/day per character
  (in-game 2026-10-23). Not worth real money; take it when it comes from pass, attendance or events
  (sources per guide [421], unverified).
- Different types stack [420]. **Scrolls can be set to auto-consume** (in-game 2026-10-23).
- Cost (own arithmetic): ~12k Kina per hour per 1,000-Kina scroll, ~50k/h for four. Cheap for boss/dungeon
  time, wasteful when kept up while idle or travelling. Superior Courage (+15%) is an Alchemy craft, tradeable [420].
- Combat Speed does speed up casting ("Attack Speed and Spell Action Speed", in-game 2026-10-23); details
  `cleric-skills.md` "Stats".
