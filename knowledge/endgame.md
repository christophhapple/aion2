# Endgame content (global/EU, level cap 45)

Researched 2026-10-10. Global launch 2026-10-05 (early access 2026-09-30). All entries are from guides and
unverified unless tagged `in-game`. Guides mentioning level 50, Brawler, Doom difficulty or Eltnen/Morheim
are about the **Korean** version (cap 50 since 2026-07-01). Gear score (GS) thresholds differ by
100–400 between guides. Source numbers [n] refer to `sources.md`. Overview: `progression.md`.

## One-time content
- **Sealed Dungeons:** solo, 60 per region (Altgard = Asmodian [18]; Verteron = Elyos, also 60 [30, snippet]). Each gives
  15k Kina, 1,250 Enhance Stones, 2 Daevanion Crystals, 2 Wisdom Stones (skill points), 2 Wing
  Featherdown, 2 Statue Scrap and a title. A full region gives 120 Daevanion, 120 skill points, 75k stones [18].
- **Strongholds:** open-world commander + chest; 13 in Altgard [18] **vs** 15 per home region [38][41]. Each gives **2 Noble Belt Enhance
  Scrolls** + 15k Kina [18].
- **Regional quests** (shown in green in the game, per the user 2026-10-10): **one-time, not
  repeatable** [92]. Reportedly 116 in total, 58 per faction (one source). Rewards: Wisdom Stones (skill
  points), Daevanion Crystals, gear, titles, Kinah, materials [92]; some give Clash Runes [19][20].
  Guides: finish one-time permanent power first, then move to repeatable content (expeditions) [92][93].
- **Enemy faction's map via Spacetime Rifts** (from level 45): Elyos get into Altgard, Asmodians into
  Verteron [94][95][96]. Unlock: accept all **7 rift quests from Mino** (Elyos; Uru in Altgard for
  Asmodians), then enter the purple portal within its 10-min window; you get **1 hour** on the enemy
  map. Rifts open every 3h (times: `schedule.md`) [94][95].
  - **Mino appears at different spots in Verteron and gives different quests at each**, so you have to
    visit several to collect all 7 (in-game 2026-10-11). ~~Only at the Dawn Legion Temporary Outpost~~
    [94][96]. Accepted quests also show under Regional Quests in the journal.
  - **Each rift quest gives a skill point** (in-game 2026-10-11), plus a Daevanion Crystal, XP, bound
    Kinah, Abyss Points, materials [94][96]. Clearly worth doing.
  - **Enemy-region Sealed Dungeons give no skill points and no Daevanion points**, but **15k Kina,
    1,000 Abyss Points and 2,500 Enhance Stones** each (in-game 2026-10-13). ~~Same bundle as
    home-region ones, another ~120 + 120~~ was our own guess [18].
  - **Enemy-region Strongholds can be entered** (in-game 2026-10-13). On 2026-10-11 none showed an
    entrance; why it differed is unknown. One gave **15k Kina + 3,000 Abyss Points** (in-game
    2026-10-13); **no belt scrolls** (in-game 2026-10-13). ~~Belt scrolls there~~ [95]; no scrolls [41] was right.
  - **Both are one-time:** rewards only on the first clear (in-game 2026-10-13).
  - **Enemy-region feathers are visible but can't be collected** (in-game 2026-10-11). Maybe unlocked
    later or different in KR: unverified.
  - Field bosses there drop gear for skins/supply requests [95].
  - **After the 7 quests:** clear the enemy Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds once each (yields above,
    one-time). After that, repeatable: Kina, Abyss Points, Enhance Stones from mobs, field bosses, green side quests; no yields published [94][96]. Titles: "Hello There" (3 rift uses), "Nice to Meet You" (50 uses, PvE Damage +2.9%, weaker than Guardian +3%) [109]. Time-extension tickets (+10 / +30 min) exist [96]. KR has a Domination mode and weekly rift challenges (Sep 2026), not seen on global.
  - Safety: open PvP there. Attacking an enemy guard flags you for PvP for 10 min. Stay unflagged and
    away from enemy outposts: their guards attack on sight [94].
  - **Kibelisks on the enemy map:** enemy Kibelisks are guarded by strong NPCs that kill you if you get
    close, and Kibelisk fast travel isn't available there; walk, fly or mount (in-game 2026-10-11;
    [105]). **Kibelisk Summoning Stones** let you place your own Kibelisk anywhere; they seem rare
    (in-game 2026-10-11). That likely explains the guide claim that you respawn at "your Kibelisk" on
    the enemy map [96?]. Without one, dying reportedly sends you home and ends the trip until the next
    rift [94][105] (unverified).
  - Getting home: automatic after the hour [95]. Early: Map (M) → circle icon top right → home region
    → use a teleport scroll to a fast travel point [94].
- Find them: Map → Exploration tab (the enemy region is counted separately from your own, in-game 2026-10-10) → Sealed Dungeon / Stronghold sub-tab ("?" icons, Travel button) [18].
  Rifts open more [14].

## Pantheon
Researched 2026-10-19 from guides [326] (global, planner), [327] (global), [328] (summaries); unverified unless tagged `in-game`.
- **Unlock:** second Ascension quest, *The One Who Hears the Voice* [327] (in-game 2026-10-19; one guide: around Lv 22 [328]). Per
  character. **Presets 1–3** (in-game 2026-10-19), part of Batch Preset Equip (`controls.md`).
- **Slots: 1 Colossus, 4 Statues, 12 Artworks** (in-game 2026-10-19); a piece fits only its own slot type. Use a decoration item once to
  add it to your list; each counts once per character; **only placed pieces count** [326][327][25].
- **Upgrade = replace** a placed piece with a better one, no enhancing (in-game 2026-10-19) [25][328]. Rarity (green → blue → purple → gold)
  sets the strength [328].
- **God stats:** each piece adds points to 1–4 of 10 god stats; **each point = +0.1% to two combat stats** (in-game 2026-10-19) [326]:
  Justice (Defense, Perfect Chance) · Freedom (Accuracy, Evasion) · Illusion (Cooldown reduction, Endurance
  Penetration) · Life (HP, Regeneration) · Time (Combat Speed, Double Resist) · **Destruction (Attack**, Perfect Resist) ·
  **Death (Critical Hit**, Regeneration Penetration) · **Wisdom (MP Cost reduction, Double Chance)** · Destiny (MP,
  Endurance) · Space (Move Speed, Block).
- **Points per piece** [326]: Colossus 35–55 per stat (70–165 total) · Statue 6–12 per stat (6–36) · Artwork 2–5
  per stat (4–20). A full faction set ≈ 427 points. Twelve top Artworks (166) roughly equal the best Colossus (165).
- **Notable pieces** [326][327]:
  - Colossus: **Guardian Lord Nahma** (Justice/Time/Destruction +55), drops from Nahma (Abyss, Fri/Sun 21:00, PvP zone) ·
    **Zikel's Apparition** (same stats +45), **14,000 Phantasmal Fragments** in the Nightmare shop · Incarnated Warding
    Feather (Justice/Time +35), Elyos quest Zumion's Ward.
  - Statues: **Transcendent Bakarma** (Freedom/Death/Space +12), Draupnir **Conquest** clear achievement (tier 4 [326]
    vs "10 clears" [327]): **10 Conquest clears** (in-game 2026-10-19) · Fortress Guardian Notun, Gerod (+10 to two stats), 8,000 Phantasmal Fragments each ·
    3 Ascension statues (Dream Fragment, Artifact of Eternity, Asahr's Mirror), one per Ascension step.
  - Artworks: **Immortal / Eternal Gartua** (Illusion/Life/Wisdom/Destiny +5): field bosses on Immortal Isle (Altgard)
    and Eternal Isle (Verteron), or Substance Morph from 20,000 Artwork Scrap. The **Substance Morph "Decor" tab** is
    these Pantheon Artworks (user 2026-10-19). Others from field bosses (piece or
    scraps), Conquest (each expedition has one, `expeditions.md`), Market, Shugo Festival shop [327].
- **Season pieces:** ranking rewards (Abyss, Nightmare, Transcendence) are decorations whose effect lasts until the
  end of the following season [326].
- **Guide picks** [328]: Colossus first (biggest single source); Statues Death, Freedom, Space; Artworks Wisdom and
  Illusion, then Destiny or Life.
- **Pieces are rare** (in-game 2026-10-19): few choices per slot early on, so filling every slot matters more than picking stats (inference).
- **GS: none.** Raises Combat Power through its stats, not GS (in-game 2026-10-19; matches [14]).
- **So (inference):** for a PvE Cleric the useful stats are Destruction (Attack), Death (Crit), Wisdom (Double
  Chance), Illusion (Cooldown), Time (Combat Speed). Cheapest real step: Zikel's Apparition (14,000 Fragments)
  competes with Daevanion crystals, Stigma Shards and Clash Rune chests in the same shop.

## Duty Missions
Researched 2026-10-11. Guides only, unverified unless tagged `in-game`.
- **Where:** Journal (J) → Duty tab, which has **two sub-tabs: Missions** (the 5/day duties below; what
  players call "duties") **and Commands** (Command Scrolls you buy) (in-game 2026-10-11).
- **Commands = Command Scrolls**, bought from a Command Merchant, 4 rarities (in-game 2026-10-12):
  - **Town (capital) merchant: Common only**, 15,000 Kina; its missions are in **Verteron** (PvE).
    Separate scroll and separate limit from the Abyss shop. **12 per week** (in-game 2026-10-12; matches [123]).
  - **Abyss merchant** ("Command Shop", Latesran Western Root scrolls, missions **in the Abyss**, PvP zone): **all four**: Common 15,000 ·
    Rare 37,500 · Epic 75,000 · Unique 150,000 Kina; **5 per rarity per week, per server** (resets with
    the weekly reset) (in-game 2026-10-12; matches [80], 20/week [123]).
  - Common reward: **1,000 Abyss Points** guaranteed + random extras: more Abyss Points, Enhance Stones,
    **Soul Crystals** (one run: +400 AP, +1,000 stones) (in-game 2026-10-12). Higher rarities' rewards: not checked. Crafting and pet materials too [123].
- **Cap:** 5 rewarded missions per day; per server [107] **vs** per account [106]. **Doesn't
  bank: unused missions are lost at the daily reset** (in-game 2026-10-13; matches [106]). ~~Banks up to 20~~ (one checklist) is wrong.
- **You can only have 5 accepted at a time** (in-game 2026-10-11), so choose; you can't grab the whole list.
- **Missions:** kill X enemies, do an activity, go to a spot, sometimes a tougher enemy; all five in
  under 30 min [106][108]. Up to 15 shown at once [108]. Refresh the list for Kina: 1,000, rising
  with each refresh [108].
- **Rewards:** each shows up to 5 rewards; slot 1 is guaranteed, slots 2–5 are chances [106].
  Types: Soul Crystals (Genus Insight, CP), Odyle Energy, Nightmare/Ascension Trial tickets, Kina,
  Abyss Points, materials [106][108]. Older figure: 50,000 Kinah + 1,000 Abyss Points each [50].
- **Pick by slot 1:** Soul Crystal > Odyle Energy > Nightmare/Ascension tickets > Kina [106]. Odyle
  Energy means more cubes, so it's stones for F2P.
- **Eternal Isle** (Verteron, Elyos): duty location ("Eternal Isle: Protect the Daeva", "…: Scary
  Spirits", 10 kills → 1,000 Abyss Points) with Lv ~51 mobs. Route: teleport to **Aullaeu Village Sky
  Island** (~1.7–1.9 km away [161]), then a **wind stream** carries you to the island, so flight gauge
  isn't an issue (in-game 2026-10-12).
- **Crimson Isle** (Verteron, Elyos): has its own teleport statue, **Crimson Isle Outpost**; mobs
  ~100–270 m from it, Lv ~46. Duty "Crimson Isle: Source of Danger", 20 kills → 1,000 Abyss Points [161].

## Pets
- Mobs drop **pet souls** on kill; they don't go to the inventory and can't be hovered. **Progress is
  only visible in the Pets menu** (in-game 2026-10-11). No loot-popup count either. **Targeting a monster shows
  nothing about its pet either**: a mob whose pet is at Lv 3 looks the same as one whose pet isn't (only the genus
  tag, e.g. "Fera") (in-game 2026-10-19). **Souls keep dropping after Lv 3** (in-game 2026-10-19). They give **no Genus Insight EXP** (in-game
  2026-10-19; ~~each soul gives Genus Insight EXP~~ [343]). The soul count stays at 105 too (in-game 2026-10-19), so **extra souls are wasted**: stop farming a pet at Lv 3. No sign they bank for a
  future level cap (KR has Lv 5).
- Pets menu → a pet's Source tab shows which monster drops its soul [121]. Drop rate falls when the mob
  is more than ~10 levels below you [121]. **Souls: 5 unlock a pet, +25 for Lv 2, +75 for Lv 3 = 105** [207][201]; cap Lv 3 on global (5 in KR/TW) [204]. ~~5 per pet~~ [121] was unlock only; numbers changed in a KR patch
  (Jan 2026), global unclear.
- Every pet owned adds small permanent stats; collection milestones at 5 pets, then every 10 [115][121].
- **Genus Insight** (pet families): 5 boards (Cogni, Fera, Natura, Varian, Special), each with 9 stat
  lines. Insight level n opens line n, up to Lv 9; higher level = better grade odds per roll [156][157].
  The highest level reached counts for all characters on the server [157].
- **Analyze** rerolls random stats on all open, unlocked lines. Cost 4 Soul Crystals (name in-game 2026-10-12; of that genus or
  plain) + 1,000 Kina; locking lines raises it steeply (1 lock 8 / 2,000, 3 locks 36 / 9,000, 8 locks
  396 / 99,000) [157] **vs** in-game 2026-10-13: 4 + 1,000 per unlocked line, double per locked line (see
  "Pets: value vs effort"), so 9 lines with 8 locked ≈ 68 crystals + 17,000 Kina per press (own arithmetic).
- Insight EXP: new pet 500, pet Insight Lv 2 and Lv 3 +250 each, **+100 per line analyzed** [157], so
  analyzing also levels the board.
- EXP to next level / grade odds per roll [157]:

  | Lv | EXP to next | Odds per line |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 500 | C 90, R 10 |
  | 2 | 1,500 | C 70, R 30 |
  | 3 | 3,000 | C 55, R 45 |
  | 4 | 5,000 | C 50, R 45, E 5 |
  | 5 | 10,000 | C 45, R 45, E 10 |
  | 6 | 20,000 | C 40, R 40, E 20 |
  | 7 | 40,000 | C 35, R 34, E 30, U 1 |
  | 8 | 70,000 | C 30, R 30, E 35, U 5 |
  | 9 | 100,000 | C 25, R 25, E 39, U 10, H 1 |
  | 10 | max | C 20, R 30, E 30, U 15, H 5 |

  Total to Lv 10: 250,000 per board. A fully raised pet gives 1,000, so pets alone reach roughly Lv 6–7
  (pets per genus not checked); the rest has to come from Analyze. Each roll gives 100 per open line,
  so the same 4 crystals give 9× more EXP at Lv 9 than at Lv 1 (own arithmetic from [157]). Stat
  values per grade: not found.
- Guide advice: level every board to 10 and ignore the stats along the way, lock nothing before 10.
  At 10, reroll unlocked until line 4 shows the best stat, then lock [156]. Soul Crystals also come
  from duty quests [106] and hidden cubes [115].

### Pets: value vs effort (researched 2026-10-13, guides, unverified)
- **Owned effect per pet is tiny** (Lv 1/2/3): Cogni HP 10/20/30, Crit +1/+2, Might +1 · Fera mount speed,
  Accuracy +1/+2, Dex +1 · Natura Stamina · Varian mount sprint cost, Evasion +1/+2, Con +1 [201][203][206].
  A main-stat point ≈ +0.1% [201]. No Combat Power figures anywhere; ~~"~55k CP for all pets"~~ [115] unsupported.
  No source says pets raise GS (inference: they don't).
- **Collection milestones are the real free value:** 100 Soul Crystals at 5, 10, 20 … 190 pets; 10 Empathy
  Candy at 50/100/150/200 instead; ~1,700 crystals total [202][203][206].
- **Genus lines** (the other real value) [201]: slot 4 PvE Damage Boost 2–4% or genus/board damage; slots
  1/5 Attack; 2/8 HP +1.6% / Defense 160; slot 7 Damage Tolerance / Regen / Status Resist. Heroic 1.5–3%,
  Unique 1–2%. Epic from Insight 4, Unique 7, Heroic 9 [208][206]. All 5 boards at 10 → title "Unbound by
  Genus" (equipped +4.5% PvE damage, +34 Attack, +68 Crit; owned +1% PvE damage) [201][208].
  **9 lines** (in-game 2026-10-16; [202][205][201]). ~~10~~ [204] is wrong.
- **Effort:** **200 pets** (Pets menu shows x/200, in-game 2026-10-13; [207]; 207 incl. 7 Special [203]); 158 drop souls [203]. Elyos: 113 farmable in the
  home region, 39 only in the enemy region (rift), 5 in the Abyss, 43 boss/chest pets (Black Cloud
  Merchant ~100k Kina each) [201]. Camps near Lv 45 (Elyos): Garden of the Illusion God (Lv 44–45),
  Eternal Isle (Lv 49), 4–7 pets within 50 m each [201]. Time per pet: not found (chance drops).
- **In-game 2026-10-13 (Cogni board, Insight Lv 4, 4 lines open):** lines rolled "Cogni Accuracy 15",
  "Cogni Critical Hit 13" (green = Rare), "Cogni Critical Hit Resist 7", "HP 10". **Analyze cost shown: 16
  Soul Crystals + 4,000 Kina**, i.e. **4 crystals + 1,000 Kina per open line; one press rolls all unlocked lines; a locked line costs double (8 + 2,000)** (in-game 2026-10-13; crystal cost re-confirmed in-game 2026-10-17: 4 per unlocked line, +4 extra per locked line). ~~4 + 1,000 flat~~ [157]. Genus-prefixed
  stats apply to you **only against enemies of that genus**: tooltip "Increases Damage when attacking
  Cogni." (in-game 2026-10-13). Plain lines (HP) always apply. The character stat window has a **Genus**
  section per family: Attack, Defense, Accuracy, Evasion, Critical Hit, Critical Hit Resist, Block
  Penetration, Block, Damage Boost, Damage Tolerance (in-game 2026-10-13).
- Max everything: ~42k Soul Crystals + 10.5M Kina [201]; rerolling for one Heroic line ~240 presses [201].
- **Empathy Candy:** transform into your equipped Lv 3 pet for 5 min (movement/range, not damage); not
  for levelling [209][203]. Every pet is a mount; Lv 2 adds gliding, Lv 3 transform [206][201].
  Auto-loot: a Kina voucher (500k / 30 days) with an equipped pet [206] **vs** pets loot [204].
- Order [201]: analyze Fera/Natura/Varian to Insight 5, then farm Cogni → Natura → Fera → Varian to Lv 3.
  Healer-specific stat picks: none found; HP% and Damage Tolerance suit a Cleric (inference).
- **So (inference):** worth doing passively: collect pets met while questing, claim milestone crystals,
  push boards to Insight 5–10 without locking. Skip heavy rerolling (Kina), rift/Abyss pets, boss-pet purchases.

### Genus Insight stat lines (researched 2026-10-16, guides, unverified)
- **9 lines per board** (in-game 2026-10-16), opened at Insight 1–9; Insight 10 adds no line, only better grade odds
  [287][288][289][291]. Grade odds: Lv 9 C 25 / R 25 / E 39 / U 10 / H 1 %, Lv 10 20 / 30 / 30 / 15 / 5 % [288][289][294].
- **Lock cost contradiction:** guides say flat 4 crystals + 1,000 Kina, 1 lock 8 / 2,000, 8 locks 396 / 99,000 [288]
  **vs** in-game 2026-10-13: 4 + 1,000 *per open line* (see above). In-game wins; recheck once more lines are open.
- **Which line rolls what: guides contradict each other.** Odealo warns KR/TW pools differ from global [292].
  - **Global (most likely)** [287 "Global BIS"][292]: lines **1, 5** Max Attack > Attack Bonus · **2, 8** HP % /
    defensive · **3, 6, 9** Crit or Accuracy > Power Shard Damage Bonus · **7** (PvE) Damage Tolerance > Endurance ·
    **4 = the board's special line**: Cogni Critical Damage Boost · Fera Weapon Damage Boost · Natura Multi-Hit /
    Perfect Chance · Varian Front/Back Damage Boost · Special Double Chance (fallback on each: (PvE) Damage Boost).
  - KR/TW layouts [287 "Asia BIS"][290][286]: Double Chance on lines 3 and 9, Damage Tolerance / Regeneration on 6,
    Boss Attack on 1/4/7 or defensive on 1/4/7. Don't use these for global.
  - Lists are "best picks", not full pools (393 properties in total, not listed [288]). Genus-prefixed stats
    ("Cogni Critical Hit") also roll (in-game 2026-10-13), so pools are wider.
- **Healer stats: none.** No source lists Healing Boost, Incoming Heal, MP, MP regen, Cooldown Reduction or
  Combat Speed on Genus lines. Closest is Regeneration (KR layout).
- **Genus-prefixed stats only work against that genus** of monster [286][290] (guides; not tested). Skip them
  unless you farm that genus [286]. Monster count by genus (global DB): Cogni 2,120 · Varian 594 · Fera 575 ·
  Natura 332 · Special 49 [293]. Boss genus breakdown: not found (one raid boss checked: Varian).
- **Strategy (guides agree):** don't lock before Insight 10 (some say 9), except a high Unique/Heroic on line 4;
  roll unlocked to level the board. At 10: lock line 4 first, then 1/5, then 3/6/9 [287][292]. Line 4 value:
  Special (Double Chance) > Varian > Fera > Cogni [287]. Board order: Cogni first (most pets), then Fera, Natura,
  Varian [292]; all five to 10 for the title Unbound by Genus. Save rainbow (universal) crystals for Special [291][292].
- **Shared per server** across characters (highest level reached counts) [290][291][295].
- **Cleric:** no class-specific guidance found. Own inference: with no healing lines, take Attack (1, 5; heals scale
  with Attack), Damage Tolerance (7), HP (2, 8), and PvE Damage Boost or the board stat on 4.

## Daevanion points: can you fill every board?
**No, not on global for now** (guides, 2026-10, estimates).
- **No Daevanion presets**: one allocation for everything (in-game 2026-10-17). Boards can be reset.
- Cost to complete [126]: Nezekan 134, Zikel 134, Vaizel 134, Triniel 168 = **570 for the four PvE
  boards**; Azphel (PvP) 232 more = **802 for all five**. Node costs 1–4 points by grade [127]. One guide
  mentions a sixth board, Ariel (PvE) [127]; not in the global client data [126].
- Points available (estimate from pre-launch datamine) [115]: levels 136 + home Sealed Dungeons 122 +
  regional quests 58 = ~316, plus Shugo shop 50 and Nightmare shop 50 (**Nightmare shop: Daevanion Crystal 300 Phantasmal Fragments, 50 per character**, in-game 2026-10-13;
  reset period not shown) =
  **~416**. Rift quests add a few. So ~150 short of the PvE boards: prioritize (see class notes).
- **Corner nodes (orange, grade Unique):** 4 points each, **+1.5% of one headline stat**, same for every
  class [314] (guide, 2026-10-17, unverified). Reaching one costs ~14–19 points incl. the path:
  - Nezekan: 2× Combat Speed, 2× Cooldown Reduction · Zikel: 2× Damage Boost, 2× Damage Tolerance ·
    Vaizel: 2× Critical Damage Boost, 2× Critical Damage Tolerance · Triniel (6 corners): 3× Multi-hit
    Chance, 3× Multi-hit Resist · Azphel (PvP): 4× PvP Damage Boost, 4× PvP Damage Tolerance.
  - Guide priority: skill nodes of your core rotation first, then route to corners; early on, paths ending
    in a corner or skill node are the best value per point [314]. Cleric guide: Cooldown Reduction + Combat
    Speed (Nezekan), Damage Boost/Tolerance (Zikel), Crit Damage (Vaizel) [111].
- **Multi-hit Resist:** "Reduces target's Multi-hit Chance", i.e. it lowers the chance that an
  attacker's hit on you becomes a Multi-hit. Daevanion has 3 nodes per class, +4.5% total [313]. No guide
  rates it; whether PvE monsters Multi-hit at all: not found. Purely defensive and small, so low priority for
  PvE (inference). Other "Resist" stats of this kind: Double Resist, Perfect Resist, Critical Hit Resist [313].
  (guide, 2026-10-17, unverified)

## Stigma levels: can you max all four?
**In theory roughly yes, but only after buying out every shop: a long grind** (guides, 2026-10).
- **Cap 20 on global** (client data); KR went to 25 with Advanced/Superior Stigma Shards [128].
- **Shards per level:** 1–5: 1 each · 6–10: 2 · 11–15: 4 · 16–20: 8. Totals: Lv5 = 5, Lv10 = 15,
  Lv15 = 35, **Lv20 = 75**; four Stigmas at 20 = **300** [128]. Shards per level rise (in-game 2026-10-11).
- **Sources (per character)** [128]: character levels ~30 (23–39: 1 each, 40–45: 2 each) + 1 Ascension
  reward · **Abyss shop: up to 200**, 4 steps of 50 at 10k / 15k / 20k / 25k Abyss Points each (in-game 2026-10-13) (cheapest
  source) · **Nightmare shop: 35** at 1,500 Phantasmal Fragments each (in-game 2026-10-13: "Per Character x/35" counts purchases, **lifetime, no reset**) · **Shugo Festival shop: 35** at
  300 Centuryroot Tokens each (in-game 2026-10-13; the game's name for the Shugo shop currency, in-game 2026-10-11; "Shugo Coins" in guides is likely the same currency). Max ≈ **300**, i.e. just enough for 4 × 20.
- **Breakpoints:** new effects unlock only at Lv 5/10/15/20; levels in between only raise the base
  values a little (in-game 2026-10-17). So shards are best spent to reach the next breakpoint.
- **Reset is free and per Stigma**: shards come back and can go into another Stigma (in-game 2026-10-17).
  Levels are **per preset**: one Stigma can have different levels in different presets (in-game
  2026-10-17). **Each preset spends the full shard pool on its own** (in-game 2026-10-17), so a
  second preset costs nothing.
- Usual approach: one key Stigma to 20, the others at 5/10/15; the last 5 levels cost 40 of the 75 [128].

## Hidden Cubes (researched 2026-10-19)
- **Where (global):** open world in **Verteron and Altgard only** (Eltnen/Morheim are KR/TW); not in Poeta, Ishalgen
  or Reshanta [340][341]. Spawn groups: **95 in Verteron, 59 in Altgard**; each group picks one of 2–43 candidate
  spots, so the exact place varies; it can come back elsewhere [341]. **No map or minimap marker**: found by sight,
  often on cliffs, ledges, ruins, riverbanks, some underwater [340]. **Sound:** cubes hum (user: they make a sound,
  2026-10-19); guides: it plays through **Sound Effects** (no own slider), heard up to ~130 m. Setup: Master + Sound
  Effects 100%, lower Music, Ambient, Monster, Mount, own character and wing sounds; headphones with 3D audio for
  direction. Which category carries it is the guides' reading of the client, unconfirmed. The angel statue near
  Spirit's Rest (Verteron) sounds similar [342]. Respawn timer: none in client data, unknown
  [341]. Fan maps with all candidate spots: metabot.gg/en/aion-2/hidden-cubes [341].
- **Key: Hidden Cube** needed for every cube in Verteron/Altgard ("Cannot open without a key") [340][341].
  Keys: hero quest Odyle Collection (Elyos) · district quest Nine Wonders 2nd Leg · achievement "Who Brought the
  Chest?" (7 across tiers) · **Season Shop: 10 per account, 3,000 Oath Coins each** · also battle pass, Monolith,
  attendance, maybe duties (amounts unknown) [341].
- **Rewards with a key** [340][341]: 10 Silentium, ~1,000 Soul Crystals, Superior Power Shards, **Soul Codex**,
  consumables; rare: wings (Radiant Love), Unique skins, emotes. Opening takes ~4s.
- One source says they're not on global yet [340]; the in-game Soul Codex source list names Hidden Cubes (in-game
  2026-10-19), so they exist.
- **So:** for a player who mostly does duties and instances, **finding cubes is the limit, not keys**: keys pile
  up, cubes are rarely passed (user 2026-10-19). The candidate spots are known (fan map), only which spot is
  active is random. Best combined with open-world time anyway (gathering, pet hunting): check the map for spots
  along that route (inference). Probably KR/TW content or a different global name;
  ignore until it shows up.

## Legions (guilds)
Researched 2026-10-13; guides only, thin sources. [196][197] read client data and mostly agree.
- **Unlock:** faction story quest ("Renewed Resolve" Elyos / "Buried Atrocities" Asmodians), from Lv 10
  [196][197][198]. One faction, one server; up to 128 members, 4 officers [196][197]. Joining is free;
  Legion menu → search/apply or accept an invite [197][198]. Leave → 24h before joining another [198, single source].
- **Join reward:** achievement "The More the Merrier": 3,000 Kina + title "There's No 'I' in Team"
  (equipped HP +270, owned PvE Attack +2) [196].
- **Passive stats by legion level** (1–20), not member count: EXP gain +1% → ~+3%; at Lv 20 HP +80,
  MP +70, Evasion +5, Accuracy +5, Attack +4, Defense +4 [196][197]. Small. ~~No bonuses at all~~ [198]
  contradicts itself. How legion EXP is earned: not found.
- **Shop / currency:** Legion Merchant sells only potions for Kina; client data shows no way to earn or
  spend Legion Coin [196] **vs** coin from legion missions, sieges, rifts, check-ins → cosmetics, mounts,
  gear [200, low reliability, likely KR]. **No Enhance Stones, Stigma Shards or Daevanion** found anywhere.
- **Hall:** rent 1M Kina / 30 days (legion pays); decorations give stats; crafting stations, storage,
  Material Conversion [196][197]. Warehouse 20 → 330 slots [197].
- **Content:** no legion quests or legion weekly reset in client data; the 4 "Legion Content" airship
  instances only have training dummies [196]. Expeditions use cross-server matchmaking, no legion needed [197].
- **Obligations:** none enforced by the game; rules are per legion (some track attendance) [199].
- **Finding one** (2026-10-18, guide, unverified): legions coordinate on **Discord**; in-game legion chat is mostly
  quiet (KR) [317]. In-client finder (KR) searches by name only, no filters for size, activity, time zone [317].
  Listings: guildorder.com legion finder (by faction + region) and LFG board [317]; no official recruitment board.
  Look for posts naming faction, server, play times and rules; prefer recent ones (many are pre-launch) [317].
  In-game options: Legion menu search/apply [197][198], or ask in region chat (general MMO practice, not from a source).
- **So (inference):** joining a relaxed legion costs nothing and gives a title + small stats; it doesn't
  help Enhance Stones, shards or Kina. Main value later: people for group/Abyss content.

## Arena (PvP, in-game 2026-10-18)
- Arena window, tabs: **Arena of Solitude** (1v1: win by defeating the enemy first within the time limit),
  **Arena of Cooperation** (5v5: win by defeating the whole enemy team), Rank, Trade Shop. The **Trade Shop is the
  same as the Abyss shop** (Stigma Shards etc. for Abyss Points) (in-game 2026-10-18).
- Season ranking: everyone starts at **1,500 RP**; promotion/demotion apply when ranks update. Season shown as
  2026-09-30 09:00 – 2026-12-16 09:00.
- **Rewards, per arena:** participation **300 Abyss Points**, victory **500 Abyss Points**. Weekly maximum: 10
  participation rewards + 30 victory rewards, reset Wednesday. So up to 3,000 + 15,000 = **18,000 AP per arena per
  week** (own arithmetic; assumes a win pays only the victory reward).
- **Matchmaking: always available, every day, both arenas** ("Matchmaking Time" tooltip). The 10v10 "Arena of Tactics" isn't in this window;
  it is the **Battlefield**, see below.

## Battlefield (10v10 PvP, in-game 2026-10-18)
- Own **Battlefield** window, tabs **Arena of Tactics** and Trade Shop. "A 10 vs 10 battle conducted under equal
  conditions, where victory is determined by following the rules of each game mode."
- **Current mode: Odyle Shard Cradle.** Guides: push the Shard Catalyst into the enemy camp within 10 minutes [322][323].
- **Matchmaking: daily 11:00–14:00 and 19:00–21:00** (in-game tooltip). Party queue only as a full 5-player party
  (in-game note); solo queue otherwise.
- **Rewards:** participation **1,000 Abyss Points + 1 item**, victory **3,000 Abyss Points + 2 items** (grey icon,
  same item as in some Season Mission rewards; guides say medals [322]). Weekly maximum **3 victory + 3
  participation** rewards, reset Wednesday. So up to 9,000 + 3,000 = **12,000 AP per week** from 6 matches (own
  arithmetic; assumes a win pays only the victory reward).
- Counts for the weekly Season Mission "Participate in Battlefield" (Challenge tab), a chain with steps at **1 / 2 / 3
  matches** (in-game 2026-10-19), so 3 matches a week finish it. ~~"1 time"~~ was only step 1.
