  2026-10-18): "09/30/2026 09:00 – 12/16/2026 09:00".** The 09:00 end doesn't fit 07:00 UTC in winter time (08:00 CET):
  either the game shows a fixed summer-time offset or the season ends 08:00 UTC. Check the countdown in December. "16:00
  server time" in [223][224] is the same moment in KST. `schedule.md` has "Dec 15 at maintenance"
  for the Daeva Pass [65][66]: likely the same maintenance window. Check the in-game countdown.

  (Abyss, Transcendence, Nightmare) [229][230].
- **Arcana:** only **Transmutation** "resets or changes" each season (in-game tutorial quoted by [158]).
  Nothing says cards or Mysterious Crystals reset.
- **Future (one source, unofficial)** [223]: S2 2026-12-16 → 2027-02-28 (adds "Subjugation"), S3
  → 2027-03-31, S4 → 2027-04-27. No official Season 2 announcement found.

## What happens at season end

Global: guides only. **Oath Coins don't carry over** [235]. Season reward claim window: "until
2027-03-10 07:00 UTC" [222] vs "through 2027-02-28" [223]; what it covers is unclear.

KR pattern (official notice S3 → Chapter 1 [229]; S1 → S2 [228]; S2 → S3 [230], AI-written post):
- **Lost:** Oath Coins (old Season Shop removed at the transition); Daeva Pass shop coin **2 weeks later**.
- **Replaced by new versions (old ones expire):** Nightmare tickets, Shugo Festival reward keys, Abyss
  Rift Stones, Unknown Rift tickets, Odyle Energy, Awakening tickets. Expedition and Transcendence
  tickets deleted (S3 → Ch1) but carried over at S1 → S2 [228].
- **Kept:** Phantasmal Fragments, Centuryroot Tokens, Abyss Points. Nightmare, Abyss and Shugo shops
  get new stock, purchase history **not** reset.
- **Gear, enhancement, Daevanion:** no source says they reset; KR added transfer systems instead
  (tuning inheritance in S2, Hero succession + succession crafting in S3 [239]).
- Ranking rewards were paid in the next season's Oath Coins [228].

## What KR added each season (preview, may differ for global)

- **S2 (2026-01-21)** [228][232][233]: new expeditions, raids, Nightmare and Transcendence content,
  an extra Arcana slot, class rankings, Abyss middle layer, tuning inheritance; target GS ~3,000.
- **S3 (2026-04-08)** [239]: GS 3,000 expedition, Hero succession (3 uses/season), succession crafting,
  catch-up boost pass + "Shugo growth boosting", Nightmare instant-clear ticket; ranking rewards
  became cosmetic.
- **Chapter 1 (2026-07-01, seasons renamed "Chapters")** [229][240, snippet]: level cap 45 → 50,
  Stigmas to level 25, new dungeons at level 50 / GS 3,500.

## So, before 2026-12-16 (inference)

- **Spend all Oath Coins.** Mysterious Crystals and Training Arcana look best for an F2P PvE player.
- **Finish Season 1 achievements**, especially Nightmare and Transcendence (the big PvE coin sources).
- **Use season-tagged consumables** (Nightmare tickets, Shugo keys, Odyle Energy, Rift Stones) rather
  than hoarding them past the season; Fragments, Tokens and AP are safe to keep.
- Season Missions each week; points don't carry over.
