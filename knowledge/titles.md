# Titles

Researched 2026-10-11 from guides reading the global client [109][110]. Unverified unless tagged `in-game`.

## How they work
- **Three effect slots:** one Attack, one Defense, one Other (utility) title. Equipped = full bonus [109][110].
- **Owned bonus:** every unlocked title also gives a small permanent bonus, equipped or not, so swapping
  is free and collecting pays off [109][110].
- The display title next to your name is set separately [110]. ~530 titles in total [109].
- Locked titles show their bonus and unlock method in the Titles menu [110].

## Easy extras for owned bonuses [109]
Join a Legion ("There's No 'I' in Team", +3,000 Kinah) · first Exploration / Conquest clear of each
party dungeon (Sightseer / Tour Guide titles) · Shugo Festival: play each minigame 3 times (9 Rare
titles) · Spacetime Rift 3 uses ("Hello There", in-game 2026-10-13) · Golden Glow: take a gear piece to max upgrade [110].
