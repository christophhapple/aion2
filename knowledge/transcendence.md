# Transcendence dungeons

Researched 2026-10-16. Entry and reward data from global client datamines [300][301]; boss mechanics mostly
from allthings.how stage-2 write-ups (region not stated, probably global) [304][305] and KR/TW-based guides
[302]. Unverified unless tagged `in-game`.

## Basics
- **Live on global (Season 1): Deus Research Base and Shattered Arkanis** [300][301][302]. Later:
  Submerged Life Temple (GS 2400+), Mirror of Scarlet Desire (3200+), Abyssal Horn Den (3800+) [300][301].
- Party 2–5 (in-game 2026-10-19), Lv 45 [300][302][304]. Stages per dungeon: **GS 1600 / 1900 / 2200 / 2500** (in-game 2026-10-16).
- **Kina per run: 300k at stage 1, +60k per further stage** (360k / 420k / 480k), paid on the boss kill, not from the cube (in-game 2026-10-18).
- **Counters:** **no Transcendence reward count on global** (in-game 2026-10-18; ~~+1 / 12h, max 14~~ [300][301]) · per dungeon 2 / day, max 4 ·
  final-boss kills 28 / week · shares the **10 unobtained cubes / week** with Conquest (leave without opening
  the cube = −1; at 0 locked out until Wed reset) [306]. Cube 40 Odyle (in-game 2026-10-19).
- **Final boss is locked behind a kill counter**; a mini-boss counts ~45 kills [304]. Deus stage 4: 150 [309, unverified].
- **Time limit:** not documented on global; 30 min on Deus stage 4 per one video [309]. A weekly
  achievement asks for a clear under 15 min [308]. What happens at timeout: not found.
- **Deaths:** respawn at a Kibelisk between bosses and glide back; mid-fight resurrection works [305].
  Falls and some mechanics can make a death unrecoverable [302]. Revive limit: not found.
- **Score:** rank + time, penalties for deaths; Rank Points count toward season rewards [300][304].

## Deus Research Base (Arcana: Bell, Compass, Chalice) [304][302][309]
1. **Gelcos**: marked player gets charged; lead it away and drop the electric zone at the edge. Pull-in →
   whip AoE (step out or defensive). Nine-lane ground slam, then a second. Fight in a corner.
2. **Nazmun**: rotating lasers (jump them), sequential beams, bombs. **Marked players get a ring that explodes
   after ~3 s and can stun: move away from the group.** Fight near the edge. Repeated laser hits drain healers [302].
3. **Atiel (final)**: shrinking arena (edges break), knockback orbs, outward waves to jump. Chains between
   players: split apart. One player trapped: others **stack to share damage**. Blue chain to boss: stay
   close. Grab-smash can one-shot. **At ~20–30 % HP "Morphic World": 10 s stagger check, fail = wipe.**
   Save stagger skills for it.
- Common wipes: failed stagger, falling off, chain mistakes.

## Shattered Arkanis (Arcana: Mirror, Parchment, Chalice) [305][300][302]
1. **Siliator of Deceit**: chakrams from 75 %: two marked players steer theirs into each other; they come back
   at 60 %. Donut + cleave: melee behind, ranged at the edge. Bouncing blades punish tight stacking.
2. **Ruthilis of Pain**: quadrant cleaves leave **lightning puddles that move clockwise and kill on
   contact**: keep rotating to the next safe quadrant. Circles → delayed shockwave rings (jump). Leaps on the
   farthest player for a donut slam: stand under it. Guillotine lines from 50 %. **Heavy cleaves on the tank.**
3. **Talisra of the Void (final, hardest)**: coloured droplets (purple/white) at 75 % and 30 %: assigned players
   soak their colour; **wrong colour stuns**. Colour-sequence domes: enter in order, keep them apart. **At
   50 % a light spear / white pillar must be destroyed fast, or the room wipes.** Diamond Jail traps a player
   (break it with directional projectiles). Tracking circle on one player: kite it away. Has an enrage (timer unknown).

## Healer notes
Nothing Cleric-specific found. From the guides: stop people taking repeated Nazmun laser hits; save MP and
cooldowns for Atiel's final phase; finish movement/colour mechanics before long casts; expect big tank damage
on Ruthilis [302][305]. **Cleanse targets:** Nazmun's stun ring [302] (Radiant Recovery removes up to 2
debuffs, `cleric-skills.md`). Whether the Talisra wrong-colour stun can be cleansed: not found.

## Rewards [304][305][300]
- Final boss: Epic Arcana (16.9 % each of that dungeon's two slots), Rare Chalice 11.2 %, Amplify Stone
  Fragment 20 %, Lesser Manastone / Soulstone 30 %, Intermediate 6 %; plus Silentium, Soul Codex, Enhance
  Stones, Kina. Mini-bosses: Soul Codex 40 %, Amplify Stone Fragment 20 %. Higher stages: bigger loot table,
  better Arcana; Soulstones from stage 4 [300].
- **So for Arcana slots:** Deus → Bell + Compass, Arkanis → Mirror + Parchment, both → Chalice.
- **Season 1 achievements** [308]: first clear per stage per dungeon 1,460 / 2,510 / 4,080 / 5,660 Oath Coins
  + Training Arcana (Training Arcana confirmed as Transcendence **Season** Mission rewards, not weekly, in-game 2026-10-19) · weekly: 3/6/10 clears, no-death clear, clear under 15 min, 10 mini-bosses, extract 10
  Arcana · 38,720 Oath Coins in total.
