# Abyss (Reshanta)

Researched 2026-10-10. Unverified unless tagged `in-game`; several sources were only readable as search
snippets. Sources [n]: `sources.md`. Schedule (sieges, bosses): `schedule.md`.

## PvP: yes, unavoidable
- **Entering the Abyss makes you a PvP target.** Both factions share Lower Reshanta and can attack on
  sight; no PvP flag or opt-out found [80][82][85]. (Being killed while flying around is expected; in-game 2026-10-10.)
- KR has a PvP on/off switch, but only for Spacetime Rifts, not the Abyss [86, KR].
- **No Stagger gauge on players:** an enemy player shows no Stagger bar when targeted, so Stagger damage does nothing in PvP (in-game 2026-10-19).
- **Safe point (Elyos): Latesran Western Root** in one corner (bind point, Command Merchant, Monolith,
  Kibelisk); the Asmodian base is in the opposite corner [80][81]. "Fixed safe islands" and choke islands
  are mentioned [82, KR/TW snippet]. **Western Root is safe: NPC guards, enemies can't get in** (in-game 2026-10-18).
- **Death: no penalty**; you always respawn at the same point, the faction's only Kibelisk (one per faction)
  (in-game 2026-10-18).
- **Abyss time: 7h per week** (in-game 2026-10-18; per layer, F2P [80][50]), runs only while you're inside. **Unused hours don't carry over; the weekly reset refills it** (in-game 2026-10-19). Shown in the
  Abyss screen and next to the minimap while inside (in-game 2026-10-18).
  **It keeps running whether you're alive or dead** (in-game 2026-10-18). Rift Stones add +1h (lesser +30 min) [50].

## Low-risk PvE in the Abyss
- **Duty Missions:** 5/day, journal Duty tab; details in `endgame.md` [50].
- **Command Scrolls (Abyss):** Command Shop in Latesran Western Root, 4 grades: **15,000 / 37,500 / 75,000 /
  150,000 Kina** (white / green / blue / gold), **5 per grade per week, per server**, reset at the weekly reset
  (in-game 2026-10-18). Each hunt pays 1,000 Abyss
  Points [80]. 27 duty scroll quests, suggested for levels 46–49, also drop Abyssal Power Shards [80].
  **Abyss Command Scroll, Common** (in-game 2026-10-18): basic reward 1,000 Abyss Points + 200 Abyssal Power
  Shards; plus 1–5 random from: Abyss Points 200 / 400 / 1,800, Enhance Stones 500 (×2 slots).
  **Green (37,500)** (in-game 2026-10-18): e.g. "Archipelago of Siel's Wings: Defeat Abyss Drakan Chief" (boss + surrounding
  forces). Basic 1,000 Abyss Points + 400 Abyssal Power Shards; random rewards: 200-stacks of green/teal crystals, a
  compass-like item, and a chance at a **Radiant Ruby** (Unique gem, crafting material) (in-game 2026-10-18). **Redraw** up to 10 times, 1,000 each (icon looks like Abyss Points).
  **Epic = 75,000, Unique = 150,000** (in-game 2026-10-20).
  **Epic** (in-game 2026-10-20): e.g. "Sulfur Tree Island: Defeat Abyss Nagarant
  Brawler" (boss + surrounding forces). Basic 1,000 Abyss Points + 600 Abyssal Power Shards (red shard icon; 200 /
  400 / 600 across grades); random rewards: 200 yellow crystals, 550 Abyss Points, a pair of Rare (green) boots,
  Fera Soul Crystals (red badge on a weapon icon, blue background; no amount shown, in-game 2026-10-20), 2,000 Enhance Stones (gold ore icon, in-game 2026-10-20). Redraw 0/10, 1,000 each.
  **Unique** (in-game 2026-10-20): e.g. "Reshanta's Root: Defeat Abyss Drakan Superior/Elite Drakan" (Elite + Superior
  Drakan + surrounding forces). Basic 1,000 Abyss Points + 800 Abyssal Power Shards; random rewards: 400 and 500
  Enhance Stones, Fera Soul Crystals (red badge, sword icon, no amount shown), 1,100 Abyss Points, 200 red crystals (teal background).
  Redraw 0/10, 1,000 each. **Abyssal Power Shards** work like normal Power Shards: toggle on to consume them, they buff Attack while being
  used up; the Abyssal ones also ignore some of a PvP enemy's Defense (in-game 2026-10-18).
- **Abyss Points cap: none shown in-game** (in-game 2026-10-18). ~~Weekly cap, separate for PvE and PvP~~ [50] not seen.
- **Shop:** Stigma Shards (up to 200 per character, **4 steps of 50: 10k → 15k → 20k → 25k Abyss Points per shard**, in-game 2026-10-13, reconfirmed 2026-10-21; next step unlocks once the previous 50 are bought; [128]; purchases count per character, "x/50" per step) and Azphel Daevanion Crystals (**10,000 AP each**, in-game 2026-10-21) [80]. Stigma details: `endgame.md`.
- **Spend Abyss Points on Stigma Shards** (player's verdict 2026-10-11). Shards per Stigma level rise with the level (in-game 2026-10-11); exact numbers not recorded.
- **Ereshkigal Monolith** (in Western Root, max level 20): uses Reshanta feathers only; gives permanent
  **PvP** stats + Azphel Daevanion Crystals (crystals confirmed in-game 2026-10-21) [80][89][90]. Traces needed: 200 [80] **vs** 300 [89].

- **19 ranks**, low → high: Trainee, Ninth- … First-Class Soldier (9), 1- … 5-Star Officer (5), General,
  Marshal, Commander, Supreme Commander [354][355]. Trainee = starting rank, no reward [355].

  limited seats [354][355]. Which points count is unclear: [354] says separate Ranking Points from Abyss PvP and
  objectives (not the spendable Abyss Points; PvP deaths can lose points; arena not mentioned), [355] just says
  "Points". The season window's Abyss panel shows Class Rank + Points (in-game 2026-10-18, `seasons.md`).

## Artifact Siege and timed Abyss content (researched 2026-10-18)

Guides mostly describe KR/TW; global details may differ. Sources [319]–[321].

- **When:** Mon, Thu, Sat 21:00 server time, 30 min (Sat + Mon in-game; times in `schedule.md`). **Countdown:** "Next Siege
  in" on the Chaotic Lower Reshanta card in the Abyss screen (in-game 2026-10-18).
- **Where:** three Artifacts in Lower Reshanta: **Reshanta's Root**, **Branding Island** (Sulfur Tree Island),
  **Grave of Steel** (Archipelago of Siel's Wings) [319][320]. Entry: level 45 + GS 1000 [319] (500k
  Combat Power in one guide, see Layers: unlikely).
- **How:** when the siege starts, **Dimensional Cores** appear at each Artifact; the faction landing the
  **final hit** on a core takes that Artifact [319][320]. A set number of players on the winning side get
  Abyss Points by contribution [319]; KR switched to per-class ranking on 2026-07-01 [320].
  **Taking part showed no contribution score or ranking anywhere** (in-game 2026-10-19, Elyos lost 1 : 2). Whether
  taking part pays AP at all: not seen yet.
- **Map panel "Artifact Siege"** (in-game 2026-10-18): shows the current owner of each Artifact since the last siege
  (score, e.g. 1 : 2, and "Faction Status: Eastern Camp Dominated"). Listed as **Reshanta's Root**, **Sulfur Tree Island**
  and **Archipelago of Siel's Wings** Artifact. Your own faction's Artifacts show **"Corridor Usage Time"** (02:10
  seen; it doesn't count down while you're elsewhere in the Abyss, in-game 2026-10-18; probably the time you can
  spend inside the corridor, counting down only in there: inference), enemy-held ones "Unavailable". In the Abyss the
  **Asmodians are called "Eastern Camp", Elyos "Western Camp"** (in-game 2026-10-18).
  Snapshot 2026-10-18: Asmodians held Root + Siel's Wings, Elyos Sulfur Tree Island. Same result after the Mon
  2026-10-19 siege (1 : 2).
- **Holding Artifacts:** whole faction gets +2.5% / +5% / +7.5% Abyss Point gain for 1 / 2 / 3 held [319].
- **Abyss Corridor entrance: map icon, you can teleport to it**, and it was open well after the siege (Sun
  afternoon after Sat's siege). It can be camped: enemy players guarded it and killed anyone entering (in-game 2026-10-18).
  **Inside: an arena where monsters spawn continuously; more kills = more Abyss Points.** One run gave ~10k AP
  (in-game 2026-10-18), ~13k AP the next time (in-game 2026-10-19), i.e. about one Stigma Shard from the Abyss shop: very worth it when your faction holds an Artifact.
  **One run used the whole 02:10 at once; afterwards the map shows "usage time expired"** (in-game 2026-10-18). So
  it is a per-siege allowance. **02:10 came without taking part in the siege** (in-game 2026-10-18), so there is a base
  allowance for the holding faction. **Joining the siege didn't add time: 02:10 again** (in-game 2026-10-19, holding 1
  Artifact; ~~joining adds 5 min~~ [319][62][64]). Open: does holding 2–3
  Artifacts raise the time? **Likely one corridor per Artifact**: each Artifact row in the map panel has its own
  corridor status ("Corridor Usage Time" vs "Unavailable") (inference from the panel, 2026-10-18; 02:10 seen while holding 1).
- **Abyss Corridor:** after the siege, an entrance opens at each held Artifact for the owning faction,
  **5 minutes** to enter and farm [319][62][64]. A faction holding none gets a catch-up corridor; AP there
  doesn't count toward the PvE cap [321].
- **Siege bosses** follow at 21:30 (Tamasa etc., `schedule.md`). Other timed Abyss bosses: Watcher Kaira every
  3h, Guardian Lord Nahma Fri/Sun 21:00, Argo the Spirit King (probably 12h after its last death). **The Abyss boss timer list shows their
  countdowns; it lists more bosses once more of the map is uncovered** (in-game 2026-10-18);
  whether it also shows the siege: unknown.
- **For a healer (inference):** the siege is mass faction PvP; joining in a group and staying near the
  front line healing can earn contribution AP, but the guides give no healer-specific advice.

## Proof of Hero → Merit Chests (researched 2026-10-19, guides, unverified)
- **What:** boss-specific materials from the Abyss bosses: Proof of Hero (Kaira), (Argo), (Nahma) from those
  bosses' rewards; Proof of Hero: Lower Reshanta from the monsters after the Artifact Siege [329][330]. Not received after a siege boss kill, but only while away respawning at the kill, so inconclusive (in-game 2026-10-19). Special grade,
  bound, stacks to 1,000, can't be sold [329][331]. **Expire when the season ends** (in-game 2026-10-19; Season 1 ends 2026-12-16 09:00 local, `seasons.md`).
- **Use:** Substance Morph (Medal tab) → that boss's Merit Chest, 100% success, no Kina [332][333]. Limits are
  **per character per week** and given per recipe; **each grade has its own limit** (in-game 2026-10-22).

| Chest | Base | Rare | Epic | Unique | Limit/week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kaira's | 1 | 3 | 5 | – | 10 |
| Argo's | – | 1 | 3 | 5 | 7 |
| Dimensional (Lower Reshanta) | – | 1 | 3 | 5 | 2 |
| Nahma's | – | 1 | 3 | **10** | 2 |

- **Contents known** [331][334]: Argo base: 1,000 AP + 1 Silver Medal of Merit + small chance at Argo gear ·
  **Argo Unique: 4,500 AP + 3 Silver Medals** + Argo gear ~0.1% each · **Dimensional Unique: 7,500 AP + 3 Silver
  Medals** + Argo/Kaira gear · **Nahma Unique: 20,000 AP + 3 Silver Medals** + Nahma gear, 7% Potential Stone: Abyss
  (Unique) (one older page lists a different Nahma chest: 6 Platinum Medals + 10,000 AP). Rare/Epic contents: not found.
- **Cheap vs expensive chests** (community advice via the user 2026-10-22, unverified): craft the cheapest grade as
  long as its weekly limit allows. Fits Argo's numbers: 1-proof chest 1,000 AP + 1 Medal per proof vs Unique 900 AP
  + 0.6 Medal per proof (own arithmetic from [331][334]); Unique keeps a better gear chance. **Kaira's and Dimensional:
  same pattern, cheap chests give more per proof** (in-game 2026-10-22, exact amounts not recorded). Nahma's cheap
  chests: contents unknown, so not checked. **Each grade has its own weekly limit** (in-game 2026-10-22),
  so: cheapest grade to its limit, overflow into the next grade.
- **Grades compared (in-game 2026-10-19):** **AP per proof is the same for every grade**; higher grades have
  slightly better odds on the random items. So a Unique = the same AP as the equivalent Rares, a little better luck,
  and fewer of the weekly crafts used.
- **So (inference):** proofs keep until the season ends, so saving up for the highest grade costs nothing
  and is slightly better, as long as you **craft everything before the season ends**. Because of the weekly craft
  limits you can't dump a big stack in the last week, so craft as you go (e.g. each time you have enough for a Unique). It matters most where the weekly craft limit binds (Dimensional and Nahma: 2/week).
  Silver Medals are PvP gear currency (low value for a PvE player).

## Silver Medal of Merit (researched 2026-10-20, guides, unverified)
- **Currency, spent in the Arena/Abyss Trade Shop** on **Centurion skin pieces**: 7 armor pieces (incl. cloak)
  40 each, necklace + earrings 80 each, bound, 1 per character [357]. No stats listed; cosmetic. Buying needs
  Ninth-Class Soldier rank [354]. "Used to craft/upgrade Abyss gear" [358] reads like Aion 1 info (Blood Marks,
  Mithril), doubtful.
- **Sources:** ~19 Abyss PvP/hunting achievements (1–20 each) [357], Merit Chests (Argo/Dimensional/Nahma, 1–3
  each, see above), weekly achievement "Daeva of Glorious Deeds" (3) (`achievements.md`).

## Advice for a healer at GS ~1100 (inference, not from a guide)
- Worth it in small doses: Duty Missions, Command Scrolls and feathers **near Western Root**, then leave.
- Go with a damage-dealing partner at minimum; a Cleric alone can't kill an attacker [85].
- If jumped: cancel flight (free-fall), press V to fly again [80]; never fight with an empty flight gauge [82].
- Bosses, sieges and contested spots need a group or legion [81][85]. Wait for more gear.
- KR cut healing in Aug 2026 (range 40 m, power −20–50%) [91, KR]; may or may not come to global.

## Supply Requests (보급 의뢰)

**Not Abyss content** (in-game 2026-10-18): the game calls them just "Supply Requests"; one system, no
separate Abyss version seen. ~~"Abyss Supply Request"~~ is a guide label [213], likely because they pay Abyss Points.
Kept in this file for the AP link.

Researched 2026-10-14. **Low–medium confidence**: no official global page; English guides don't say
which version they describe; detailed sources are KR (2025-11 → 2026-05). Sources [211]–[221].

- **What:** hand in specific items, get **Abyss Points + Enhance Stones** (in-game 2026-10-18) [211][213]
  (xboxplay says Kina + AP [212]). PvE-only way to earn AP [211][214]. Same requests for everyone [215].
- **Where:** Esc menu → Supply Requests (in-game 2026-10-18); KR: ALT+J [217]. Unlock condition not found.
- **Tabs (in-game 2026-10-18):** Emergency (daily), Weekly, Craft, Boss. **Craft and Boss are not repeatable**: one
  hand-in per request. Emergency asks for different items every day and resets at the normal daily reset
  (in-game 2026-10-18). Guides: Craft/Boss stay up long-term
  ("Season: …" in KR [216][219], "Regular: …" in a global guide [214]; some stay up all season [215]).
  Weekly resets at the normal weekly reset, Wednesday (in-game 2026-10-18).
- **Hand-ins:** Silentium for the repeatable ones (in-game 2026-10-18) [211][212][213]; gear of a required grade (more AP for
  higher grade) [216]; crafted gear and materials for the Craft tab [214][218]; field boss gear for
  the Boss tab: keep unneeded boss drops for it [221]. No list of which items qualify.
- **Silentium rewards** (expcarry, "Global", 2026-10-12 [211]):

  | Request | Silentium | AP | Enhance Stones |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Emergency | 5 / 8 / 10 | 980 / 1,640 / 2,240 | 250 / 420 / 570 |
  | Weekly | 20 / 30 / 50 | 3,210 / 5,410 / 9,980 | 820 / 1,390 / 2,560 |

  KR (2025-11) paid less: 5 → 750 AP, 8 → 1,260 [218]. Another guide: "380–6,000 AP per delivery" [214].
- **Silentium sources** [211][212]: Exploration expedition 4, Conquest 7, Transcendence cube 10, Hidden
  Cube 10, Daeva Pass. Stacks to 1,000. Bound, can't be traded. **Only uses found** (2026-10-14): supply requests, or NPC sale for 5,000 Bound Kina each (not usable on the Market); no crafting or shop use mentioned [211][213]. So keep it for requests.
- **Caps / banking:** none found. KR blog: up to 5 daily requests, ~500 AP each [217] (low reliability).
  Unclear whether this AP counts toward any AP cap [220, snippet].
- **F2P note:** KR players buy cheap crafted gear on the Market to turn in [218]; on global, Market
  buying needs membership [220], so for F2P only own drops/crafts and Silentium count.
- **So (inference):** spend Silentium here instead of selling it; check the Emergency tab daily and
  the Boss tab before salvaging field boss gear.
