# Expeditions

Researched 2026-10-10 (global client); unverified unless tagged `in-game`. Sources [n]: `sources.md`.
Guides write "item level"; the gates below are GS.

## The six live expeditions

Three tiers, each a pair: one drops **weapons + accessories**, the other **armor** [46][48].
Every expedition has **exactly two modes: Exploration (easy) and Conquest (hard)**; no third/Hard mode
(in-game 2026-10-10). **Changed with patch 2026-10-21:** Krao Cave and Draupnir get a Hard mode, see below.

### Hard mode, Krao Cave + Draupnir (patch 2026-10-21 [377][378], not seen in-game yet)
- Entry **GS 2600**; boss Frenzy after 4 min; 3 revives per player.
- Rewards: chance at Philosopher's Stone: Revelation (Bound, also selectable as the guaranteed reward),
  Ariel Fragments (Bound, type per dungeon), chance at Expedition Hero Artwork, more Kina than Normal, all
  Unique crafting materials (Wrathful Mind / Will / Ego).
- Substance Morph: 100 Ariel Fragments → 1 Daevanion Crystal: Ariel (Bound), 30× per character; or
  1 Fragment → 5 Enhance Stones, no limit. Feeds a new **Ariel Daevanion board** (per-dungeon point cap).
- New achievements: Expedition Hero Artwork Collector, Expedition (Hard) ones with Unique skins.

## How rewards work

**Clearing is free; Odyle Energy pays only for the reward cube** [47].

| | Exploration | Conquest |
|---|---|---|
| Reward counts | **No clear limit** (in-game 2026-10-19). ~~7 per dungeon, refill to 7 on Wednesday~~ [46][50] | **None on global**: no Conquest reward count, only the Odyle cube cost (in-game 2026-10-18). ~~+1 every 8h, bank up to 21~~ [46][47][50] |
| Cube cost (Odyle) | Krao, Urugugu **20** (Krao in-game) · Fire Temple **30** · Draupnir, Vakron, Horn Den **40** [46][49][51] | **40** (in-game) |
| Weekly cube limit | **none** (in-game) | **10 "unobtained cubes" per week**: clears left without opening the cube (in-game 2026-10-12); shared with Transcendence (in-game) |
| Final boss, guaranteed | 4 Silentium + **1,000–1,500 Enhance Stones** [49] | 7 Silentium + **2,000 Enhance Stones** [49] (1,000–2,000 [56]) |
| Kina per Conquest run | – | **Urugugu, Vakron ~300k each** (in-game 2026-10-18); **Fire Temple, Ferocious Horn Den ~400k each** (GS 2100) (in-game 2026-10-18). **Paid on the boss kill, not from the cube**, so no Odyle needed (in-game 2026-10-18). The biggest regular Kina source for F2P |
| Pity (runs to guarantee) | Krao, Urugugu, Fire Temple 1 · Draupnir, Vakron, Horn Den 3 (in-game) | ★1 Krao, Draupnir: 14 · ★2 Urugugu, Vakron: 21 · ★3 Fire Temple, Horn Den: 28 (in-game) |

- **Remaining Unobtained Cube Count:** 10 per week per server (Wednesday reset), shown on the Conquest
  view. Tooltip: clearing and leaving *without* obtaining the Odyle cube rewards lowers the count; at 0
  you can't enter Conquest **or Transcendence** (in-game 2026-10-12; ~~only a warning [50]~~). Not
  Exploration (in-game). So it's a cap on runs without spending Odyle, **not** a target to use up.
  Clears where the cube is opened (Odyle spent) do **not** lower it (in-game 2026-10-12).
  ~~10 cube opens per week~~ (our earlier reading). Likely purpose (own guess): Conquest also drops gear
  and wing feathers outside the cube, and this stops farming those without spending Odyle.
- Odyle Energy: +15 per 3h = 120/day (= 3 cubes at 40, or 6 at 20). Cap 560 (840 is membership, paid);
  overflow is lost [47][49][51].
- **Recharge items** (e.g. Odyle Energy (Bound), +40 each) fill a **separate pool, Charged Odyle**, shown as
  "60 (+300) / 840". Cubes spend the normal pool first, the charged pool after, so normal regen keeps running
  while charged Odyle is held (in-game 2026-10-23). Season-bound items expire, see `seasons.md`.
- Every Conquest drops ≥1 feather for that boss's wing (Substance Morph) [45]. Sealed wings convert to
  Enhance Stones, capped 20/week per server (patch 2026-10-07) [7].
- Chance drops: Amplify Stone Fragments, Soul Codex, Wrathful Mind; Unique/Heroic gear "small chance"
  [45][46][49]. Once you own a dungeon's set, cubes give mostly stones [51].
- Each expedition has an Artwork (Pantheon piece) and a sealed wing [49].

## Exploration or Conquest at max level? (analysis, from the numbers above)

**Odyle Energy (120/day) is the real limit**, so compare stones per Odyle:

| Cube | Odyle | Stones | Stones per Odyle |
|---|---|---|---|
| Krao / Urugugu Exploration | 20 | 1,000–1,500 | **50–75** |
| Fire Temple Exploration | 30 | 1,000–1,500 | 33–50 |
| Any Conquest | 40 | 2,000 | 50 |
| Draupnir / Vakron / Horn Den Exploration | 40 | 1,000–1,500 | 25–38 (worst) |

- **For stones:** Krao and Urugugu Exploration are as good as or better than Conquest per Odyle, and
  they don't touch the unobtained-cube count, which only gates Conquest and Transcendence (in-game).
- **Conquest is still needed** for what Exploration doesn't give: the IL 54 gear set, wing feathers,
  pity rewards (14 claims for ★1), and the one-time 2,480-stone first-clear achievement.
- Suggested mix: Conquest until you have the Krao/Draupnir set pieces you want, then spend the rest on
  Krao/Urugugu Exploration (20). Skip 40-cost Exploration cubes. Exploration cubes cost 20–40 (in-game 2026-10-19).

## Suggested order around GS 1100 (inference, not from a guide)

1. **Daily Dungeon, all 14/week:** by far the most stones per run, and no Odyle cost.
2. **Krao + Draupnir Conquest:** 2,000 stones each, plus 2,480 for each first clear; together they
   complete the IL 54 set (Krao weapons/accessories, Draupnir armor) [46][49][56].
3. With leftover Odyle: **Krao / Urugugu Exploration** at 20 per cube (half price, 1,000–1,500 stones).
   Whether that beats Conquest at 40 is unclear: compare in-game.
4. Next gate **GS 1400:** Urugugu + Vakron Conquest (need 2+ players). Then Transcendence at 1600.

Party finding: in-game Quick Join / Party tools (unconfirmed) [46]; third-party LFG sites exist.

**Daily Dungeons** (from level 30): three short **solo** instances, one of them Daeva Bio-Research Base
(defeat Balaur test subjects before they transform; the score sets the Enhance Stone reward) [97][98].
**Tickets: 14 per week, cap 14, refill on the weekly reset** (in-game 2026-10-10) [3][97]. Can be extended
**by** up to 30 extra tickets, probably via shop/premium (in-game; ignored, F2P). ~~7 free entries [99]~~.
