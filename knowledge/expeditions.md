# Expeditions

Researched 2026-10-10 (global client); unverified unless tagged `in-game`. Sources [n]: `sources.md`.
Guides write "item level"; the gates below are GS.

## The six live expeditions

Three tiers, each a pair: one drops **weapons + accessories**, the other **armor** [46][48].
Every expedition has two modes: **Exploration** (easy) and **Conquest** (normal) [46][47][49].

| Tier | Expedition | Exploration (Lv / GS) | Conquest (Lv / GS) | Party | Conquest loot |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ★1 | Krao Cave | 20 / 200 | 45 / **700** | 1–5 | Rupture weapons, Berk accessories (item IL 54) |
| ★1 | Draupnir | 45 / 700 | 45 / **700** | 1–5 | Bakarma armor (IL 54) |
| ★2 | Urugugu Canyon | 28 / 300 | 45 / **1400** | 2–5 | Aulamus weapons + accessories (IL 70) |
| ★2 | Vakron Sky Island | 45 / 1400 | 45 / **1400** | 2–5 | Vakron armor (IL 70) |
| ★3 | Fire Temple | 35 / 500 | 45 / **2100** | 1–5 | Enraged Kromede weapons + accessories (IL 84–86) |
| ★3 | Ferocious Horn Den | 45 / 2100 | 45 / **2100** | 1–5 | Nuakum armor, Wrathful Ego (IL 86) |

Five more exist in client data, gated at 2800–3800 GS (Hard 3000–4500): Dying Dramata's Nest, Cradle
of Nihility, Hall of Illusion, Azure Breath Island, Citadel of the Fallen Daeva. Not confirmed live [46][48].
Transcendence is a separate dungeon type, not an expedition (stages 1600/1900/2200/2500) [47][49].

**Contradictions:** Hard mode on global: Normal/Hard 700/2400, 1400/2500, 2100/2600 [45] **vs** no Hard
at launch [47] **vs** an "unlabeled third mode" at 2400–2600 [46]. Vakron gate 1400 [46][47][48] **vs** 1600
[55]. KR added Hard in Dec 2025 [52][53, KR].

## How rewards work

**Clearing is free; Odyle Energy pays only for the reward cube** [47].

| | Exploration | Conquest |
|---|---|---|
| Reward counts | 7 per dungeon, refill to 7 on Wednesday, don't bank [46][50] | +1 every 8h, bank up to 21 [46][47][50] |
| Cube cost (Odyle) | Krao, Urugugu **20** · Fire Temple **30** · Draupnir, Vakron, Horn Den **40** [46][49][51] | **40** [46][49][51] |
| Final boss, guaranteed | 4 Silentium + **1,000–1,500 Enhance Stones** [49] | 7 Silentium + **2,000 Enhance Stones** [49] (1,000–2,000 [56]) |
| Pity (claims to guarantee) | Krao, Urugugu, Fire Temple 1 · Draupnir, Vakron, Horn Den 3 [49] | ★1: 14 · ★2: 21 · ★3: 28 [49] |

- **Unobtained cube count:** 10 per week per server (Wednesday reset). A clear **without opening the
  cube still uses one** [46][49][50]. At 0 you can't enter Conquest/Transcendence [49] **vs** only a
  warning [50]. Don't clear without claiming.
- Odyle Energy: +15 per 3h = 120/day (= 3 cubes at 40, or 6 at 20). Cap 560 (840 is membership, paid);
  overflow is lost [47][49][51].
- Every Conquest drops ≥1 feather for that boss's wing (Substance Morph) [45]. Sealed wings convert to
  Enhance Stones, capped 20/week per server (patch 2026-10-07) [7].
- Chance drops: Amplify Stone Fragments, Soul Codex, Wrathful Mind; Unique/Heroic gear "small chance"
  [45][46][49]. Once you own a dungeon's set, cubes give mostly stones [51].
- Each expedition has an Artwork (Pantheon piece) and a sealed wing [49].

| Source | Stones | Limit |

All from [56] except limits.

## Suggested order around GS 1100 (inference, not from a guide)

1. **Daily Dungeon, all 14/week:** by far the most stones per run, and no Odyle cost.
2. **Krao + Draupnir Conquest:** 2,000 stones each, plus 2,480 for each first clear; together they
   complete the IL 54 set (Krao weapons/accessories, Draupnir armor) [46][49][56].
3. With leftover Odyle: **Krao / Urugugu Exploration** at 20 per cube (half price, 1,000–1,500 stones).
   Whether that beats Conquest at 40 is unclear: compare in-game.
4. Next gate **GS 1400:** Urugugu + Vakron Conquest (need 2+ players). Then Transcendence at 1600.

Party finding: in-game Quick Join / Party tools (unconfirmed) [46]; third-party LFG sites exist.
