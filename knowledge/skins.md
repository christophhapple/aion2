# Skins, Closet and Skin Combine

Researched 2026-10-22. Guides only, unverified unless tagged `in-game`. No official notice or Reddit thread
found. Sources [n]: `sources.md`. Skin sources (shops, events, achievements): `events.md`, `seasons.md`,
`abyss.md`, `expeditions.md`. Titles: `titles.md`.

## Closet
- **Closet** window: Alt+O or the main menu [417]. Skins only count once you **right-click to register** them
  [414].
- **Collection counter** (in-game 2026-10-24): top left of the Closet, a book icon with % and owned/total for
  the open tab (Armor showed 17%, 132/766). Tabs Armor / Weapons / Decoration, slot filters on the right edge
  (SET, ALL, per slot). Duplicates show a number badge on the icon and **seem not to count** towards owned
  (player's impression, not tested; matches "duplicates give nothing" below).
- **Collection Effect window** (in-game 2026-10-24): opened from the counter. Three groups with % and owned/total:
  **Weapons** x/56 (own class's weapon type only; the other 7 weapon types are listed at x/56 each with the note
  "The weapon skins of another class are not added to total collection points"), **Armor** x/766 (Helm 115,
  Pauldrons 107, Top 109, Legs 109, Gloves 109, Shoes 109, Cloak 108), **Accessories** x/117 (Earrings 59,
  Necklace 58). Each slot shows its points and bonus, **+0.1% per point confirmed** (e.g. 37 pts = +3.7%):
  weapon → that weapon's Attack, armor slot → that slot's Defense, earrings/necklace → Attack **and** Defense
  of that slot. Points per skin vary (18 skins gave 37 pts), consistent with points by grade.
- **Collection bonus:** 18 slot tracks [guides; the window shows 8 weapon types, 7 armor, 2 accessory slots, in-game 2026-10-24], 0–500 points each, +0.1% per point (cap +50%) [410][416]. First unlock
  gives Common 1 / Rare 2 / Epic 3 / Unique 4 points (Heroic 5 [416]); **duplicates give nothing** [413][416].
- Weapon tracks only boost the matching weapon type; other classes' weapon skins give no points [410][416].
  They **do count for Fashionista**: it showed 223 while the Collection Effect window added up to 217 with other
  classes' weapon skins, 175 without (in-game 2026-10-24; the remaining 6 maybe Decoration, unchecked) [414].
- **Fashionista tiers** (in-game 2026-10-24): 10 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 300 / 500 skins (Lv 1–6; higher tiers
  not seen). Rewards include Kina, 4k at Lv 1 rising to 13k at Lv 6.
- Launch shop (Atelier/premium) skins give no points [416][413].
- Worn skins give no stats, except Centurion/Decanus skins (PvP stats) [410].
- **Titles:** Fashionista (100 skins): +1.9% Combat Speed, Crit +5 [410]. **Empty Closet (700+):** +3.5% Combat
  Speed, +3.5% Cooldown Reduction, +42 HP regen equipped, +120 HP owned [410][413] **vs** +5% Combat Speed, +40
  regen, +50 HP [413, community figures].

## Skin Combine
- **Where:** Combine button in the Closet [414]; GameRant describes a window with 4 slots and a middle slot for
  a Lucky Aura [415].
- **Input:** 4 skins of the **same grade** [413][414] ("4 or more" [418, Kinah seller]). **Inputs are consumed**
  [413][415]; a sacrificed skin can be re-obtained by extracting that item again [415].
- **Output:** 1 random skin of the same grade, or with a fixed chance one grade higher [410][412][413]. Every skin
  in a grade equally likely, no pity [411, KR]. Weapon results match your own class's weapon type [413].
- **Fee and upgrade chance** (global client data) [410][412]:

  | Input grade | Fee | Upgrade chance |
  |---|---|---|
  | Common | 600 Kina | 25% → Rare |
  | Rare | 1,000 Kina | 20% → Epic |
  | Epic | 6,000 Kina | 15% → Unique |
  | Unique | 30,000 Kina | none, rerolls a Unique |

  **Contradiction:** Unique → Heroic 10% (15% with Aura) [411, KR] **vs** Unique never upgrades on global
  [410][412][413]; Heroic skins can't be combined [412].
- **Lucky Aura:** +10 points of upgrade chance (Common/Rare/Epic), +5 (Unique; effect on global unconfirmed)
  [410][411]. Season Shop 2,550 / 10,100 / 40,200 Oath Coins, 5 / 3 / 1 per account per server [410]; also
  from achievements and hidden cubes [410].
- **Can't combine:** customized (dyed) skins until the dye is removed [414]; equipped skins (unverified).
  Bound vs tradeable: not found. Duplicates-only rule: not confirmed (guides speak of spare/duplicate or
  registered skins [413][414][419]).
- **Does combining lower the Closet / Fashionista count?** Not found. One guide recommends combining cheap
  Market skins in fours on the way to 700 [414], which suggests it doesn't, but doesn't say so. Check in-game.

## Guide advice
- Combine spares for collection breadth; don't chase one specific skin (~1.25% per design even at Common)
  [410][411]. Unique combines are poor value (30k, no upgrade) [410][412].
- Lucky Auras on Epic combines [410] **vs** most useful at low tiers [411, KR].
- For Empty Closet the **count** matters, not the grade: register everything, buy cheap "(Skin: …)" Market
  items and Black Cloud Merchant skins not marked "Owned", prefer your own class's weapon and accessory skins
  [414]. Item extraction is the weakest route (~10% skin chance, 4,000 Kina) [414].
- Combine is the main source of most armor skins [412].
