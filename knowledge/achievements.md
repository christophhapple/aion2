# Achievements

Researched 2026-10-15. Mostly metabot.gg, which reads the global client (build 0.0.4387.0, pages undated)
[270]. Unverified unless tagged `in-game`. Titles from achievements: see `titles.md`.

## How they work
- **Sections** [270]: Adventure (Kibelisk, Exploration, Sealed Dungeon, Shugo Festival, Stronghold) ·
  Challenge (Field Boss, Hunting, Combat) · Growth (Social, Milestones, Quests) · Items (Enhancement,
  Crafting, Gear Collection, Gathering, Consumables) · Dungeons (7) · Season 1 (Challenges, Abyss,
  Transcendence, Nightmare, Arena). ~191 titles come from achievements [276].
  ~~Titles menu~~ categories (Adventure, Quest, Challenge, Dungeon, Item, Growth) are a separate menu (`titles.md`).
- **Tiered:** I/II/III…, each tier pays at its threshold. metabot lists only the total over all tiers.
- **Not found:** whether rewards must be claimed by hand, and whether progress is per character or
  account-wide. Check in-game.

## Duty Missions and Commands
**Where (in-game 2026-10-18):** Achievements → Progress tab → Quest (left). Shows Five Veiled Fates and Herald of Hope
with the current tier, the Supply Request chains (Emergency 50, Weekly 5, deliver Rare items 50 / Epic items 5 / Rare gear 5 /
gathered materials 150) and quest-line ones. Rewards per tier shown as Enhance Stones (gold) + Kina (silver), some
with an item; a "Collect Rewards" button per card plus "Collect All Rewards", so **claimed by hand**. Quest category
level (e.g. Level 3, 26/33) gives its own reward.
**Only count achievements**: nothing per region, per mission, or for doing different missions [271][272][273].
So for achievements **any duty counts**; pick by reward (slot 1 guaranteed: Soul Crystal > Odyle > tickets > Kina [275], `endgame.md`).

| Achievement | Requirement | Tiers | Total reward | Title |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Five Veiled Fates** | Complete Duty Quest: Mission | 3/10/30/50/100/150/200 | 3,710 Enhance Stones + 104,000 Kina (bound) | **Protector of Atreia** at 30: equipped Damage Boost +1.3%, owned Accuracy +5 [272] |
| **Herald of Hope** | Complete Duty Quest: Command | same | same | **Messenger of Atreia** at 30: equipped Block +32, owned Evasion +5 [273] |
| Daeva of Glorious Deeds (weekly, Season 1) | Complete 3 **Abyss** commands | 3/6/10 | 820 Oath Coins + 6,000 AP + 3 Silver Medals of Merit | – [274] |

Whether town (Common) Command Scrolls count for Herald of Hope: presumably, not stated. The weekly
Season one needs Abyss commands (missions in the Abyss, PvP zone).
