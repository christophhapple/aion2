# Open-world events

Researched 2026-10-11. Times: `schedule.md`. Sources [n]: `sources.md`. Unverified unless tagged `in-game`.

## Dimensional Invasion

- **When:** every hour at :30 server time; ~3 min to join (timer icon next to the minimap), then the
  round. **10-minute time limit; a round took about 5 min** (in-game 2026-10-11). ~~3 min~~ [102].
  Random field spots in the home regions (Verteron/Altgard) [100].
- **Who:** solo is fine, no minimum party [100]. Rewards from level 45 [100].
- **Three types, one shared reward table** [100][102]. Waves of mobs across a platform, boss in the
  centre near the end [102]. Beritra Air Raid seen in-game 2026-10-11.

  | Type | Mobs | Boss |
  |---|---|---|
  | Beritra Air Raid | Drakan Air Raiders, Air Raid Warmages, Ferocious Racon | Air Raiders Captain Tetrata |
  | Accursed Sword | Corrupted Drakan forces | Executor of the Magical Sword |
  | Incursion of the Spirits | Earth, Fire and Wind Spirits | Grandios, Spirit Ruler |

- **Scoring:** points from damage and kills over the whole round; the boss is a big share, so keep
  hitting it when it spawns [101][102]. AoE and DPS classes rank higher.
- **Reward picks (contradiction):** top 2 get 4 picks, top 5 get 3 [102] vs. score ≥100 gives 3 picks
  regardless of rank, <100 nothing [103, KR/TW]. Unverified for EU; not important in practice.

### Keys
- Rewards cost a **Dimensional Invasion Reward Key**. +1/day, holds up to 7 [100][102].
- **Cancelling the card selection keeps the key, but gives no rewards at all** (in-game 2026-10-11).
  So a round without a key is wasted; only join when you'll spend one.
- Refill time: "16:00 server time" [100][102], probably wrong for EU like those sites' daily reset.
  Likely the normal daily reset (07:00 UTC): player's assumption, not checked.
- Shared across characters on the server per some guides, account-wide per others (see `schedule.md`).
- One KR wiki mentions a cap of 2 reward claims/day and 14/week [103]: likely KR only.

### Rewards (global client data, level 45–49 bracket [100])
- ~~Common package without a key~~: no rewards without a key (in-game 2026-10-11).
- With a key: Enhance Stone ×700 + ×1,200, Centuryroot Token ×50, Soul Codex (Bound) ×3, Abyss Points
  ×800, plus chance drops [100]. Felt "mediocre, but worth it" (in-game 2026-10-11, amounts not checked).
- Centuryroot Tokens are spent in the **Festival Shop** (growth materials, skins) [100][101].

**So:** worth doing **only with a key**, ~1 per day; ~5 min for maybe ~1,900 stones. Small next to
the Daily Dungeon (~10,000). Keys bank to 7, so no need to go every day.
