# Special Quai Membership (subscription)

Researched 2026-10-19. Account status: per player, `players/<player>/account.md`.

## Basics
- **Price CHF 11.99 per 30 days** (in-game 2026-10-19; USD 15 [344][345]). **Applied per server**: all characters on
  that server (in-game 2026-10-19; [344]). Buying again adds 30 days; max 3 purchases per server per month, only
  while < 60 days remain [344]. On sale since 2026-10-05 [344].

## Benefits (in-game list 2026-10-19)
| Benefit | What it means |
|---|---|
| Remote Storage | Open your storage from the bag instead of a Storage NPC [344] |
| Wind Breeze Merchants | An extra shop menu; stock not listed anywhere, "may change" [344][345] |
| Max Odyle Energy 840 | Cap 560 → 840. **Regen stays +15 / 3h** as far as known, so no more Odyle per day, only less waste when you can't log in |
| Market access | **Contradiction:** without membership you can list but not buy [344] vs selling needs membership (in-game 2026-10-13, `crafting.md`) |
| Quna Exchange | Kina ↔ Quna (the premium currency) exchange [344] |
| **Odyle Energy Cube Selection Count** (Expedition, Transcendence, Sanctuary) | See below |
| Max Shugo Festival keys 21 | Cap 12 → 21; refill (+3/day) unchanged, so again only less waste |

Guides also list **Personal Trading** (5/day, both players need membership) and World Market [344]; neither is in
the in-game list. "Abyss time 14h with membership" [3] is not in the in-game list either: likely wrong for global.

## "Odyle Energy Cube Selection Count"
- After a clear you inject Odyle into the reward cube to open it. **Without membership: once per cube. With
  membership: twice** (official notice wording, via [344][346]). The second injection costs the Odyle again and
  gives a second reward roll from the same cube.
- **So:** a member gets up to two cube rewards per clear, paying Odyle for each. Since Odyle income isn't higher,
  the gain is **rewards per clear** (time saved, more out of each run), not more rewards per week from the same
  Odyle. One guide: for pity-style guaranteed drops counted per opening, this halves the runs needed (e.g.
  Draupnir 14 → 7) [346] (unverified).
- Interaction with the weekly "unobtained cube" limit (`expeditions.md`): presumably still one cube per clear,
  opened twice (inference).

## For an F2P PvE player (inference)
- The two "max" changes only prevent waste; the cube rule saves time; Market/Quna matter for trading. Nothing
  gives power you can't get without it, just faster. Guides with Odyle 840 or 21 Shugo keys assume a subscription.
