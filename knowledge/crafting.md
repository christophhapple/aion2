# Crafting and gathering

Researched 2026-10-10. Few solid global sources; best is [70] (2026-10-06). Unverified unless tagged
`in-game`. Sources [n]: `sources.md`.

## Bottom line (for a GS ~1100 player limited by Enhance Stones)
- **Neither crafting nor gathering produces Enhance Stones** (no source lists them) [70][72][74][75].
  So they don't help with the main bottleneck directly.
- Their value is **Kinah** (selling materials or crafted items), **but only with membership**: F2P can't
  sell on the Market (in-game 2026-10-13) and later **top-tier crafted gear**
  (Dragon Lord, item IL 102 per piece vs. IL 54–86 for expedition sets [70][72][76]), which is expensive.
- Gathering costs no energy (none found), so it's a reasonable **gap filler** between scheduled events.
- Substance Morph can turn an **unused Clash Rune Enhance Scroll into 100 Enhance Stones** (in-game
  2026-10-17), but it's **not worth it** (player's judgement): keep spare scrolls for the runes.

## Professions
- **Five crafts:** Blacksmithing, Armorsmithing, Handicrafting, Alchemy, Cooking [70][72].
- **One gathering skill, "Essence Extraction":** ore, gems, logs, herbs, cooking ingredients, Odyle [70][71].
- No cap: one character can level all five crafts [70]. **No need to commit to one: crafts aren't
  learned or unlearned, so there's nothing to reset** (in-game 2026-10-17). Unlock at character level 10 [72].
  Per character or per account: not stated (probably per character) [70].
- What they make: Alchemy → Manastones, Soulstones, potions; Cooking → buff food [70][72];
  Blacksmithing/Armorsmithing/Handicrafting → weapons, armor, accessories.

## Leveling
- Each craft: Novice 1–50 → promotion quest (at Mastery 50) → Professional 1–50 [70]. Essence Extraction:
  proficiency 1–100 [71].
- **Gathering promotion (Elyos):** bring a Splendent Ruby to Korius in Verteron. While the quest is
  active, each successful Ruby extraction drops one (bound) [70][73]. Ruby spots in Verteron: Fortress
  Ruins (outer wall, SE of the Dawn Legion Base), Ellun River Midstream, Destroyed Abyss Gate, Kraka
  Footpath [73].
- **After the promotion: Professional 1–50, and no further promotion quest so far** (in-game 2026-10-10,
  at Professional 50). This matches proficiency 1–100 in [71].
- Gathering skill points per proficiency level (Common/Specialty); "Proficient Handling" raises the
  success gauge (+5 → +50) [71]. **There aren't enough skill points to take every gathering skill**
  (in-game 2026-10-10), so picks should follow what you farm. Respec 50,000 Kina (in-game 2026-10-13).

## Gathering skills (Essence Extraction) [162][163][164][165]
Guides 2026-10, unverified unless tagged. Levels 6–10 (common) and specialty above Lv. 1 need Professional.
- **Points from proficiency 1–100: 95 common + 14 specialty** [162] (in-game 2026-10-13: totals match
  a fully spent Professional 50). Respec: "Skill Reset" button on the Essence Extraction screen, 50,000 Kina (in-game 2026-10-13): cheap, so re-pick freely when plans change. Maxing everything costs 120 + 42,
  so you choose.
- **Common skills** (all nodes), each Lv. 1–10, costs 1/2/3/4/5 then 5 per level = **40 to max**, 15 for Lv. 5:
  - **Proficient Handling:** success gauge +5 per level per successful roll (+50 at 10).
  - **Delicate Touch:** failure gauge −8 per level per failed roll (−80 at 10).
  - **Lady Luck:** chance to multiply a success gain ×2.5 (from Lv. 6 also ×4); 8% at Lv. 1, 24% at 5,
    40% at 10 (Lv. 8: 33%, in-game 2026-10-13). **Triggers only when the failure gauge is ≥ 50%**
    (in-game 2026-10-13 tooltip, "at a critical moment"), so it's a rescue for near-failed nodes.
    ~~Any successful roll~~ [162][164].
  - Guides: Proficient Handling + Delicate Touch first for consistency; Lady Luck is the gamble [164].
- **Specialty skills**, per material family (Odyle, Ore, Gem, Log, Herb, Ingredient), in sequence [162][163]:
  - **Seeker** (1 pt): that material on the minimap/compass. Guides: take it early for what you farm [165][164].
  - **Plentiful Harvest** (1 pt per level, 3 levels): 10/20/30% chance of double materials.
  - **Appraiser** (1 pt per level, 3 levels): 10/20/30% chance to reroll the material's grade
    (not if already higher). A full family = 7 pts.
- Demand: Radiant Odyle is in ~190 recipes, Radiant Orichalcum Ore in ~120 [162] (Radiant = very rare when gathered, see
  "Recipes and economy"), so **Odyle and Ore** are
  the safe specialty picks before a craft is chosen.
- Nodes: ~3 harvest attempts as a mini-game; failed rolls block a slot. Shared between players, respawn
  timer unknown [73][77].
- **No gathering/crafting energy or daily cap found** [70][71]; for crafting confirmed: **no energy or
  daily limit, only Kina and materials limit it** (in-game 2026-10-17). (Odyle *Energy* is the dungeon-cube
  currency; Odyle *the material* is something you gather.)

## Recipes and economy
- Recipe tiers: basic → Expert → Artisan. 25% chance of a "Splendent" (better) result on supported gear;
  Artisan Splendent results feed the Dragon Lord gear [70][76]. (80.1% figure elsewhere is probably the
  base success rate.)
- **Gathered material grades** (in-game 2026-10-13): **Odyle, Orichalcum Ore, Yggdrasil Logs, Gems** (Ruby,
  Sapphire…) come in four: Common → Fine (Rare) → Pure (Epic) → Radiant (Unique). **Herbs only Common and
  Fresh (Rare).** Cooking Ingredients: not four grades, exact grades not recorded.
- **Odyle grades: Odyle (Common), Fine Odyle (Rare), Pure Odyle (Epic), Radiant Odyle (Unique),
  the last very rare** (in-game 2026-10-13; matches four grades up to "Radiant" [72]). Radiant Odyle also
  in the Shugo Festival shop, 1,000 tokens, 3 per month. Appraiser (grade reroll) helps here. Guide
  prices: level-40 Radiant Odyle sells to NPCs for 540 Kinah,
  Radiant Orichalcum Ore for 135 [72]. Both go into Dragon Lord recipes (e.g. a helm: Radiant Ore ×2,
  Radiant Odyle ×2, Wrathful Ego ×3, Balaur leather) [72][76].
- Craft vs. buy (prices 2026-10-05) [70]: a Splendent Orichalcum Longsword costs ~223k to craft vs. 100k
  to buy; a Star Dragon Lord Longsword ~12.5M to craft vs. 37M listed. Rule: sale price minus material
  cost, fees and waste; check recent sales first.
- Gathering zones: Verteron / Altgard, Chaotic Lower Reshanta (Abyss); Odyle also inside expeditions [71].
  Both home regions have the same material types; node counts are similar (Orichalcum: Verteron 546,
  Altgard 530; Diamond: Verteron 192, Altgard 200). No Altgard-only (enemy region) material found (2026-10-18, guides, unverified) [318].
- **Crafted vs. expedition gear on global: unknown.** In Asia, crafted gear rolls max Potential stats [75];
  not confirmed for global. An old KR article said the opposite [75].

- **Mastery gates:** Expert recipes 15+, Artisan 30+, Balaur materials 51+, Dragon Lord 55–60+ [166].
- **Dragon Lord** via Transfer Crafting (Expert's → Artisan's → Dragon Lord tiers 1–3) [168]; transfer keeps
  enhancement, amplification, Soul Binding [167]. Crafted top gear: 5 Soul Binding slots vs 3–4 [168] /

  moving or being hit mid-craft = failure [167][168]. ~74% at equal mastery up to low 90s, never 100%,

- **Energy / daily limits:** none documented in any source. Unknown: check in-game.

- **Substance Morph** (Alt+H) turns Odyle into **any gathering material: Ore, Gems, Logs, Herbs, Cooking Ingredients** (in-game 2026-10-13). ~~Only Ore, Gems, Logs~~ [167][169]. Same exchange rate for every target material (in-game 2026-10-13); exact rate and whether grade carries over not recorded. So Odyle is the flexible material for any craft.

### Market (decides whether crafting can earn Kina)
- **Selling on the Market needs membership** (in-game 2026-10-13) [174][175][392][394]. ~~F2P can list and
  sell~~ [176][391]. Without membership you craft for self-use only. Buying also needs membership [390][391].
  Market from **level 45** [390][391].
- **Registration Fee = 2% of the total sale price**, paid when listing (in-game 2026-10-22: 1,595 for
  4 × 19,949 = 79,796). **Paid in either Kina; Bound Kina is used first if you have it** (in-game 2026-10-22).
- **Sell window** (in-game 2026-10-22) shows "Market Price, last 28 days": lowest, highest, average and last
  trading unit price, plus the current lowest listing; the price per item defaults to the current lowest.
  No sales tax shown in this window.
- **Sales tax: 10%** (in-game 2026-10-22) [393][395]. So a sale nets ~88% of the listed price (−2% fee, −10% tax). The current
  "Market Tax" is shown top right on the Market screen [391]: check in-game. Buyer pays the fee [397, KR, Kina seller].
- **Slots, listing duration, price floors/caps:** not found.
- **Only "Tradable" items** can be listed [391]. Crafted gear stays tradeable until equipped / Soul-Bound
  [398][399]. Higher Balaur materials are often Bound [399]. **Odyle and everything made by Substance Morph
  is Bound** [395]: it can't be sold raw, only turned into a craft whose result is tradeable (unverified).
- **Proceeds are normal Kina** (after fees); Bound Kina can't be spent on the Market [390][400]. No
  player-to-player trade on global, the Market is the only way [390][394].

## Crafting for Kina (researched 2026-10-22, guides, unverified)
Thin data: no official fee table, no Reddit threads, **no Kina/hour figure for global**. Several sources sell
Kina or boosting. Prices go stale within days; check the Market before crafting.

- **Which craft (guides):** **Alchemy** most often named for Kina: consumables and Manastones always sell, and
  Spiritstone Powder drops from mobs [403][405]. **Handicrafting** "most important profession right now",
  crafted items expensive in the launch window [406]; bows sell because Ranger is the most common class [403].
  **Armorsmithing** widest demand (7 slots, all classes) [405]. **Cooking** least profitable [403][70].
- **Manastone listings, EU / Nania** (in-game 2026-10-22, lowest listing price, number of listings):
  | Manastone | Lowest listing | Listings |
  |---|---|---|
  | Lesser | 124,990 | 18 |
  | Intermediate | 160,000 | 49 |
  | Superior | 788,887 | 18 |
  | Intermediate Abyssal | 749,998 | 4 |
  | Lesser Abyssal, Superior Abyssal | none listed | 0 |
  Asking prices, not sales. Material cost per Manastone not recorded yet, so the margin is unknown.
- **Life Crystal** (potion) (in-game 2026-10-22, 28 days): average 17,750, range 100–70,000, lowest listing 19,949.
- **Price examples (region not stated unless G):**
  - **Superior Life Serum** (Alchemy, mastery 55): listed 6,499–15,000 each; inputs Life Serum ~1,000 + Fresh
    Aria ~2,000 (can be morphed from Fine Odyle) + Glass Bottle 1,200 (NPC) ≈ **4,200** (the guide's 3,200 is
    a miscount); 72.9% success, 25% combo [395] (2026-10-17). Thin margin after 10% tax at market input
    prices; better if the Aria is morphed from your own Odyle.
  - **Splendid True Dragon Lord Ring:** materials ~14–15M, comparable listing ~27–28M [401, Kina seller].
  - **Artisan's Orichalcum gear:** cheapest listings ~90k (dagger) to ~699k (gloves) [402, G, undated].
    Artisan's and above: "tens of millions" [398].
  - Splendent Orichalcum Longsword: craft ~223k vs buy 100k [70]: a loss, so not every craft pays.
  - Manastones, potions, Orichalcum Ingots: named as steady sellers, no prices [403]. Raw ore/gem/Odyle,
    Manastone and food prices on global: not found.
- **Cost to level a craft to the profitable tier** (Kina, materials, time): not found anywhere. Advice
  only: level on cheap Common recipes (ingots), use Bound materials you already have (lowers real cost)
  [408], don't burn rare materials for mastery EXP or speculate big [407].
- **Raw materials vs crafting:** "sell raw while early-global prices are high; a fast 20k material beats a slow
  rare one" [408, Kina seller, TW prices] vs "refine into ingots/powders rather than sell raw" [407]. No numbers.
- **Pitfalls:** failure eats all materials, never 100%, no pity, no restores [398][399]; a 300M craft that sold
  for 280M [408]; Soul Binding makes gear unsellable; keep Splendent intermediates for the next tier
  [398][407]; overproduced items drop fast [404, low quality]. Bots inflating prices: KR/TW only [409].
- **Inference:** with membership the low-risk start is Alchemy consumables/Manastones (cheap inputs, mob drops,
  steady demand), checked against current Market prices; gear crafting for sale only once mastery 55+ and
  Market prices show a margin after the ~10% tax.
