## Skill level priorities (guides disagree)
- [137]: Hellfire 16 first, then Firestorm, Bittercold Wind, Blaze, Wish, Winter's Shackles, Flame Arrow;
  Ice Chain, Flame Scattershot, Defiance to 12; drop Frost and Frost Burst.
- [141]: Firestorm 12 → Wish 16, Blaze 16 → core skills to 20 (Hellfire, Firestorm, Blaze, Wish, Bittercold
  Wind); don't level everything evenly.
- [139]: Firestorm 12 → Wish, Blaze, Flame Arrow 16 → Hellfire 20.
- [142]: Hellfire > Firestorm > Bittercold Wind > Blaze > Wish > Flame Arrow > Flame Scattershot >
  Winter's Shackles (same order on the Daevanion board).
- Common ground: Hellfire, Firestorm, Blaze, Wish are the core; Frost/Frost Burst/Ice Chain are leveling skills.

## Rotation (PvE, guides)
- **Opener:** Element Enhancement → Delayed Explosion → fully charged Hellfire → Fire Wall + Cold Storm
  once the boss stands still [141][142]. [139] puts Fire Wall and Cold Storm first.
- **Loop:** Firestorm on cooldown (each fireball −2s Hellfire CD with option 4); Blaze on Fire-Marked
  targets; Flame Arrow as filler for MP and Pyroclasm; Flame Scattershot only during Stagger [137][139][141].
- **MP is damage:** keep MP ≥50% for Robe of Earth / Grace of Enhancement (see Stats); a few Flame
  Arrows refill it [137][141].
- Don't press Wish right before the boss goes invulnerable or you have to move [141].
- Macro example in [137] (Hellfire stays manual; delay 10 ms at ping <50, 40–50 ms at 80–100+).

## Stats (PvE)
- **Accuracy first, but only until your big spells stop missing** [120]; then damage. Others put
  Accuracy 4th and add it "once bosses start dodging" [119].
- Then **Attack > Critical Hit > MP** [119]; alternative order: Attack/PvE Damage Boost > Critical
  Damage Boost > Cooldown Reduction > Combat Speed [120]. HP and Damage Tolerance only as much as
  the fight needs [120].
- **MP is a damage stat** via two passives: Grace of Enhancement = +20% PvE Damage Boost while MP ≥25%;
  Robe of Earth = +105 Crit while MP ≥50% [118][119]. So never play at 10–20% MP [118].
- Manastones: Attack, Critical Hit, Accuracy if you miss [118]; Weapon Damage Boost or Critical Damage
  Boost as premium picks; Cooldown Reduction comes from Daevanion, titles, wings, not stones [120].
  Front Attack doesn't apply to all spells (DoTs, Fire Wall…) [120].
- Daevanion: Combat Speed + Cooldown Reduction (Nezekan) → Attack/Damage Boost → Crit Damage, then blue
  skill nodes (Hellfire, Firestorm, Bittercold Wind, Blaze…) and Robe of Earth; skip Multi-Hit and
  defensive nodes [118]. Metabot: Zikel first for its Grace of Enhancement nodes [119].
- Titles: same picks as any PvE character (`titles.md`).
