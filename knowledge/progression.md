# Progression map

One-page overview for a level-45 character, written for free-to-play. Details and sources:
`expeditions.md`, `gear.md`, `endgame.md`. Drafted 2026-10-10 from guides; **unverified** unless tagged
`in-game`.

## The big picture

- **Gear score (GS)** gates content. It comes from gear, enhancement (+1 GS per level, in-game), belt,
  amulet, Clash Runes, Manastones and Arcana.
- **Combat Power (CP)** is how strong you are. Daevanion board and Pantheon raise CP, **not** GS [14].
- **Enhance Stones are the bottleneck** (in-game). Enhancing +1 → +10 always succeeds, so stones turn
  directly into GS.

The loop: run content → Enhance Stones + gear → enhance → higher GS → next content tier.

| 1000 | Abyss (PvP zone) | Abyss Points; worth it from ~1600 |
| 1000 | Ascension Trial (3/week): Easy 1000, Normal 1500, Hard 2000, Extreme 2500 (in-game) | 3,500 stones per clear (difficulty not stated) |
| **1400** | Conquest ★2: **Urugugu Canyon** (weapons/accessories), **Vakron Sky Island** (armor, 1400 in-game); 2+ players | IL 70 set |

Exploration mode (easy) of most expeditions opens much earlier: Krao 200, Urugugu 300, Fire Temple 500.

## What to do around GS 1000 → 1400 (F2P)

**Every week:**
1. **Daily Dungeon, all 14 runs.** ~90k stones/week (in-game average; guides say 140k), with no Odyle cost. Nothing else comes close.
2. **Spend Odyle Energy before it caps** (560): Krao + Draupnir Conquest (40 per cube, 2,000 stones,
   gear toward the IL 54 set), or Krao/Urugugu Exploration (20 per cube, 1,000–1,500 stones).
   Conquest allows 10 clears per week *without* opening the cube; at 0 you can't enter (in-game).
   Exploration has no such limit. Always open the cube: skipping it lowers the count.
3. Nightmare (2/day, banks): Phantasmal Fragments for a spare Clash Rune.

**Once:**
4. Finish **Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds**. Each Stronghold = 2 belt scrolls.
5. First Conquest clear of each expedition: +2,480 stones (achievement).
6. **Enhance everything to +10** (always succeeds; extraction refunds 100% of the stones, in-game).
7. **Belt and amulet** to Epic +10 → transmute → Unique. 30 scrolls per item in total; check for spares.

## Watch out
- Clash Runes above +1: failure **destroys** the rune (in-game). Only try with a spare.
- Above +10 on normal gear: 65% → 20% success; failures cost materials only [21][22].
- Unique tier of belt/amulet: ~201k stones + 5M Kina for +0→+10 [38]. Plan for it.
- Spare Empyrean Traces: keep them. A stones conversion exists in KR but isn't on global yet [39][40].

## Often missed (quick wins)
Collected 2026-10-11 from guides [115][116]; check each in-game.
- **Unused Daevanion Crystals / Wisdom Stones** in the inventory do nothing until used [116].
- **Pets:** every owned pet adds small permanent stats; level each to 3 by killing mobs [115]. The
  guide's "~55k CP for all pets" seems high: unverified.
- **Ascension Bracelet** (story reward): enhance to +10–11 (adds attack + defense), put your one Superior
  Manastone in it; a second bracelet comes from a faction quest [115].
- **Manastones** in every socket of gear you keep (white 1 socket, green 2, …) [115][116].
- **Owned bonuses:** titles (`titles.md`) and wings both give permanent stats just for owning them
  [115][116]. Easy titles: Shugo Festival minigames ×3 each (9 titles), join a Legion, first Exploration
  and Conquest clear per dungeon [109].
- **Hidden cubes** in the world: pick them up (also feed Genus Insight) [115].
- Daevanion points: +1 GS each [116] **vs** raises CP only, not GS [14]. Check in-game.

## Skill points (Wisdom Stones)
Researched 2026-10-13; guides unless tagged. 1 Wisdom Stone (Bound) = 1 skill point [188].
- **One-time (F2P):** level-ups to 45 = 203 SP [186]; regional (green) quests 1 each, ~59 open-world
  [186] (24 per a datamine [188]); Spacetime Rift quests 1 each (in-game 2026-10-11); home-region Sealed
  Dungeons 2 per first clear [18]. **Enemy-region Sealed Dungeons give none** (in-game 2026-10-11), so
  the guide pool of 384 SP (203 + 122 + 59) [186] overstates one faction. Strongholds: unclear [192].
- **Monolith / feathers:** trade for Wisdom Stones [192][193] **vs** global gives only Daevanion
  Crystals there [186, newer, global-specific]. Check in-game.
- **Repeatable, the main F2P lead:** **Shugo Festival shop** (Centuryroot Tokens) sells Wisdom Stones
  in 4 price tiers, 350 / 700 / 1,400 / 2,800 tokens, 5 per tier (up to 20), reset period unknown
  [191, datamine, unverified live].
- **Nightmare shop** (Phantasmal Fragments): listed as selling Wisdom Stones [194][193], no price or
  limit found; visible global shop data shows only Daevanion Crystals [189]. Check in-game.
- **Not found:** Abyss shop, Daeva Pass free track [195], launch/Twitch events, achievements, titles,
  Legion. Higher passive levels come from Daevanion boards (+n on top of the skill-point cap of 10).
